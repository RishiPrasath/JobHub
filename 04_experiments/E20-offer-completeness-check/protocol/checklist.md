## Things the checklist deliberately does not do

- S2 says employers have 14 days from the first day of work to issue written KETs, and acceptable forms include a contract, handbook, website or circulars. S6 says KETs should be given before work starts as far as possible, and no later than 14 days after, and that at the point of job offer all relevant information on employment terms must be in place. The two are not identical. So a term missing from an offer letter is reported as "not stated in this letter", never as a breach. The term may be in the contract or handbook.
- Whether a person is covered by the Employment Act is not decided (R09 notes that coverage conditions are unverified).
- Pay date: MOM's list has "salary period" (item 7), not a "pay date". The checklist uses the MOM wording.
- No probation cap, no statutory pay-date rule and no CPF pro-rating rule are included, because none was read.
