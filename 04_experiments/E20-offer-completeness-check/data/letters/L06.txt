OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT

Dear Aisyah,

We are pleased to offer you a position with Kopitiam Ridge Trading Pte Ltd. The terms of the offer are set out below.

Your role
You will be employed as Administrative Assistant. Your main duties and responsibilities are handling purchase orders, filing, answering customer calls and updating the inventory spreadsheet. This is a fixed-term appointment of 12 months. Your place of work is our shop office at 52 Kallang Place, Singapore.

Hours and pay
Your working hours are 8.30am to 5.30pm with a one-hour lunch break, 5 days a week, and your rest day is Sunday. Your basic salary is $2,600 per month. You will receive a fixed meal allowance of $100 per month. There are no fixed deductions from your salary other than your employee CPF contribution. Any overtime pay is paid together with your monthly salary. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times your hourly basic rate of pay. You are eligible for one month's basic salary as a year-end bonus, paid in December, if you are still employed on 31 December.

Leave and benefits
Your leave entitlements are 7 days of annual leave for the 12-month contract, 14 days of outpatient sick leave, 60 days of hospitalisation leave, and maternity and childcare leave for eligible employees as set out in the staff handbook. You will have outpatient clinic visits reimbursed up to $300 a year and group hospitalisation insurance paid by the company; there is no dental benefit.

Probation and notice
You will be on probation for 3 months. Either party may end your employment by giving 2 weeks' written notice, or pay in lieu (also 2 weeks during probation).

Next steps
Please sign and return the enclosed copy of this letter by Thursday, 26 November 2026 to accept this offer. If you would like to clarify any term before you decide, please contact Mr Goh (admin@kopitiamridge.example).

We look forward to welcoming you to the team.
