OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT

Dear Wei Ming,

We are pleased to offer you a position with Harbourline Logistics Pte Ltd. The terms of the offer are set out below.

Your role
You will be employed as Senior Operations Analyst. Your main duties and responsibilities are analysing shipment and warehouse data, preparing weekly performance reports for the Operations Director, and recommending process improvements. Your employment will start on 1 December 2026. This is a permanent appointment. Your place of work is our office at 18 Tuas Avenue 9, Singapore.

Hours and pay
Your working hours are 9.00am to 6.00pm, 5 days a week, and your rest day is Sunday (Saturday is an off day). You will be paid monthly, on the last working day of each month. Your basic salary is $5,200 per month. You will receive a fixed transport allowance of $200 per month. There are no fixed deductions from your salary other than your employee CPF contribution where it applies. As a professional, managerial or executive employee you are not eligible for overtime pay. You are eligible for a discretionary annual performance bonus of up to one month's basic salary, paid in March, depending on company and individual performance.

Leave and benefits
Your leave entitlements are 14 days of annual leave in your first year (rising by one day each year), 14 days of outpatient sick leave, 60 days of hospitalisation leave, and maternity and childcare leave for eligible employees as set out in the staff handbook. You will have group hospitalisation and surgical insurance paid by the company, outpatient claims of up to $500 a year and dental claims of up to $200 a year.

Probation and notice
You will be on probation for 3 months. Either party may end your employment by giving 1 month's written notice, or salary in lieu of notice (1 week during probation).

Next steps
Please sign and return the enclosed copy of this letter by Friday, 20 November 2026 to accept this offer. If you would like to clarify any term before you decide, please contact Ms Lim in HR (hr@harbourline.example).

We look forward to welcoming you to the team.
