## Part C: read the range and its basis

Where this comes from: Singapore Ministry of Manpower, Occupational Wage Table, reference month June 2025 (published June 2026). It covers full-time resident employees (citizens and permanent residents) in all industries and the gross monthly wage excludes bonuses. Advertised pay (what a posting says), offered pay (what you are told in an offer) and paid pay (what people actually receive) are different things. This range describes what people in the occupation were paid; it is not a judgement of any employer or of your worth.
