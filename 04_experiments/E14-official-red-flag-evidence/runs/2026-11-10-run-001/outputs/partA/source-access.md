## Summary

- Part A threshold: at least one employment-relevant official source readable and permitted. MET by the MOM Employment Agencies Directory flagged list (26 agencies) and the MOM stop-work-order list (143 rows), read within their stated terms for a plain read. Caveats: (1) both are narrow: recruitment agencies' licensing, and workplace-safety orders on construction and factory sites; neither is a general employer-misconduct list; (2) the directory needs a rendering browser, has no documented machine-readable download, and MOM bars commercial reuse without permission, so building on it needs written permission or a user-run lookup; (3) the two lists name entities, so identity matching (E11) is needed before use.
- Still not usable: PDPC (terms forbid robots), SGX (does not render, terms unread), ACRA and ScamShield (caching and scraping barred, no issues list). Judgments are readable but ECT decisions are anonymised.
- Counts of fetches: about 25 reads across the sources, spaced by tool latency; the MAS source was read once, so the 2-second limit was met.
- Date of the retrieved data: 2026-11-10. The directory page footer says "Last Updated 25 Aug 2023", probably a static string; the status data looked current (agencies licensed in 2025 appear).
