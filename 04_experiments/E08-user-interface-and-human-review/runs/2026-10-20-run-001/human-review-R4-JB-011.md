# Human Review — R4-JB-011

Status: in progress

## Source observations

- Role appears to be a part-time assistant role.
- Displayed pay is S$12–S$15 per hour.
- Hours are negotiable.
- A related salary teaser refers to an Administration Officer role.
- The relationship between the part-time assistant posting and the Administration Officer salary teaser is unclear.
- The application note says that details will be explained at the interview and that only shortlisted candidates will receive further consideration.
- The posting indicates an office/on-site arrangement.
- The employment arrangement is part-time.

## Compensation ambiguity

The posting does not explain how the exact hourly rate within the S$12–S$15 range will be determined.

It is also unclear whether the related Administration Officer salary teaser applies to this role. It must not be treated as this role's compensation without verification.

## Role and process ambiguity

- The actual duties are not described in the posting.
- The posting does not explain what the applicant would be expected to do day to day.
- The application process is brief: send a resume; details are explained at interview; shortlisted candidates only.
- The posting does not guarantee that sending a resume will result in an interview.
- The company identity and its relationship to the posting should be confirmed from the source record.

## Unknowns

- What determines whether a candidate receives S$12, S$13, S$14 or S$15 per hour?
- Does experience, availability, responsibility or another factor affect the rate?
- Is the Administration Officer salary teaser connected to this posting?
- What are the actual day-to-day duties and responsibilities?
- Who is the legal employer, and is the named company the hiring organisation or a recruiter/client reference?
- What are the expected days, hours and start/end times for the part-time arrangement?
- What happens during the interview, and what information is provided before any commitment?

## Decision-changing verification questions

1. How is the exact hourly rate within the S$12–S$15 range determined?
2. Does the Administration Officer salary information apply to this part-time assistant role, or is it for a separate role?
3. What are the actual duties and responsibilities of the role?
4. Who is the legal employer, and what are the expected working days and hours?
5. What information or commitment is requested during or after the interview?

- Final decision: not yet recorded
