Job Title: Customer Service Representative  
Company: Private Advertiser  
Location: Central Business District, Singapore  
Employment Type: Part‑time  
Salary: $12 - $14 per hour  

Job Description  
We are looking for enthusiastic individuals to join our front‑line team. You will be the first point of contact for our customers, providing friendly and efficient service.  

Key Responsibilities  
- Answer inbound calls and respond to emails promptly  
- Resolve customer inquiries and complaints with empathy  
- Process orders and handle returns accurately  
- Update customer records in the CRM system  
- Meet daily performance targets  

Requirements  
- Minimum GCE ‘N’ level or equivalent  
- Good command of English; Mandarin a plus  
- Strong communication and problem‑solving skills  
- Ability to work flexible shifts, including weekends  
- Basic computer literacy (MS Office, internet browsing)  

Benefits  
- Competitive hourly rate $12 - $14  
- Flexible scheduling to fit study or other commitments  
- On‑the‑job training and career development opportunities  
- Employee discounts on company products
