Company: Pacific Horizon Technologies Pte Ltd
Location: Singapore – Central Business District
Job Title: Senior Product Manager
Posted 7 days ago   Apply Now   Save Job   Share

Job Description
Pacific Horizon Technologies is seeking an experienced Senior Product Manager to lead the development and launch of innovative digital solutions for the financial services sector. In this role you will collaborate closely with cross‑functional teams including engineering, design, marketing and sales to define product vision, prioritize features and ensure timely delivery. You will be responsible for conducting market research, gathering customer insights and translating them into clear product requirements. The ideal candidate will have a strong track record of managing complex product lifecycles, driving user adoption and delivering measurable business outcomes. You will also mentor junior product staff, facilitate stakeholder workshops and represent the product at executive briefings. A passion for fintech, data‑driven decision making and an ability to thrive in a fast‑paced environment are essential.

Qualifications
A bachelor’s degree in Business, Engineering or a related field is required, with at least eight years of product management experience, preferably in technology or financial services. Proven expertise in agile methodologies, roadmap planning and performance analytics is expected. Excellent communication and leadership skills are a must.

Benefits
Medical and dental coverage, annual leave, flexible working arrangements, professional development allowance, employee stock options, wellness programs and commuter subsidies.
