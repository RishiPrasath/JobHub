Job Title: Sales Associate
Company: BrightWave Media
Location: Singapore – Central Business District
Employment Type: Full‑time
Salary: Up to $3,000 + commission
Posted: 5 days ago
Application Deadline: applications will be reviewed until 28 November 2026

Job Description
BrightWave Media is a fast‑growing digital marketing agency focused on delivering innovative campaigns for regional brands. We are looking for a motivated Sales Associate to join our dynamic team and drive new business opportunities.

Your day to day
- Identify and prospect potential clients through cold calling, networking events and social media.
- Conduct needs‑analysis meetings and present tailored media solutions.
- Prepare and deliver compelling proposals and quotations.
- Negotiate contracts and close sales in line with company targets.
- Maintain accurate records in the CRM system and provide weekly sales reports.
- Collaborate with the creative and account teams to ensure seamless onboarding of new clients.

About you
- Minimum 1‑year experience in B2B sales, preferably in advertising or digital services.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills; fluency in English and Mandarin is a plus.
- Proven track record of meeting or exceeding sales targets.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team in a fast‑paced environment.
- Proficient with Microsoft Office and CRM tools (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce).

Benefits
- Competitive base salary up to $3,000 plus uncapped commission.
- Medical and dental insurance.
- Annual leave of 14 days plus public holidays.
- Professional development allowance.
- Flexible working hours and remote work options.

How to apply
Click the “Apply Now” button below, upload your resume and a brief cover letter highlighting your relevant experience. Our recruitment team will review applications promptly.
