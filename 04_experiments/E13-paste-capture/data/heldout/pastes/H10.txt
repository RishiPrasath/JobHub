Join Our Team – Business Development Manager (Singapore)  

InnovateTech Solutions is a leading provider of AI‑driven enterprise software, empowering businesses across Asia Pacific to accelerate digital transformation. We are seeking a dynamic Business Development Manager to drive market expansion, forge strategic partnerships, and lead the sales cycle from prospecting to closing. In this role you will identify new business opportunities, build and maintain relationships with key stakeholders, develop and execute go‑to‑market strategies, conduct market research and competitive analysis, negotiate contracts, and collaborate closely with product, marketing and customer success teams to ensure seamless delivery and client satisfaction; the ideal candidate will have a bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing or a related field, at least 5 years of B2B sales experience in the technology sector, proven track record of exceeding revenue targets, strong communication and presentation skills, fluency in English and Mandarin, ability to work independently in a fast‑paced environment, and a deep understanding of the Singapore enterprise market and SaaS business models.
