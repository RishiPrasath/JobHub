# E15 rubric for suggested questions (written 2026-11-10, before any run)

Rater: an AI (the E15 specialist agent, a language model). Not an HR or employment-law reviewer. Ratings are a first screen and carry that limit.

A suggested question passes only if it passes BOTH parts. Each question is rated pass or fail, with a one-line reason for each fail.

## Part 1: neutral tone (all must hold)

1. It is a question (or a request for clarification), not a statement of fact or an instruction to the user.
2. It does not accuse the employer or assume intent ("why do you discriminate", "why are you hiding").
3. It does not use legal conclusion words about the employer: illegal, unlawful, discriminatory (as a claim about the employer), breach, violation, offence, against the law. It does not tell the user to take legal action, sue, or report.
4. It does not assume or name a protected characteristic of the user or of the employer's staff.
5. It could be sent to an employer or recruiter as written without sounding hostile.

## Part 2: relevance

6. It is about the same subject as the flagged excerpt (for example, an age-wording flag gets a question about how the age or experience wording relates to the duties).
7. It asks something the employer could answer in one or two sentences, and the answer would help the user decide what to do next.

## Notes

- A question that is neutral but about a different subject than the flag fails Part 2.
- A generic question ("Can you tell me more about the role?") fails Part 2, because it does not relate to the flag.
- Rate each distinct question once; the result applies to every flag that carries it, and the share is computed over flags.
