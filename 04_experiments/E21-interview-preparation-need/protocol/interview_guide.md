# E21 Part A interview guide (v1, frozen before the simulated pilot)

For use with REAL job seekers (8 to 10, interviewed within the last 6 months). The same wording is given to the simulated pilot personas.
Source: the Design section of the E21 README. Question order is the README order. Questions 1 to 7 come before the posting-and-profile tool is mentioned, so earlier answers are not primed by it.

## Before starting (real participants only)

- Say the session is about how people get ready for interviews, that there are no right answers, and that nothing is being sold.
- Ask for consent to take notes. Notes carry no names or employer identities.
- Say they can skip any question and stop at any time.
- Ask them to talk about their MOST RECENT interview, not an ideal one.
- Do not mention practice tools, AI or JobHub until question 6.

## Questions

1. **Role and process.** Tell me about the most recent interview you had. What was the role, and what was the process (how many rounds, what format, one interviewer or a panel)?
2. **Preparation.** How did you prepare for it? How long did you spend, and what did you use (friends or family, videos, articles, AI assistants, mock-interview tools, a coach or agency, nothing)? Did any of it cost money?
3. **Hardest part.** What was the hardest part? Were there questions you were not ready for? Tell me about one specific moment.
4. **Surprises.** Did anything about the format surprise you?
5. **Wished-for and blockers.** Looking back, what would you have wanted before the interview that you did not have? What stopped you from preparing more?
6. **AI mock interviews.** Have you ever used an AI mock interview or a similar practice tool? What was it like? Did you carry on or stop, and why?
7. **Recording and storage.** How do you feel about a practice tool recording your voice or video? How do you feel about where your answers would be stored?
8. **Posting-aware tool.** Would you practise inside a tool that knows the job posting and your profile? Why, or why not?

## Probes (use neutrally, only when an answer is thin)

- "Can you give me an example?"
- "What did you do next?"
- "What happened when you tried that?"
- Never offer an answer ("was it hard to explain your switch?"). Do not name S-23, S-24 or S-25 or any story. Let the participant raise them.

## Pilot-only additions (simulated personas only, not part of the real-participant guide)

- **8N. Neutral version of question 8.** "Thinking about the last interview, if you had to get ready for the next one tomorrow, what would you do, and what would you want help with, if anything?" Used to test whether the wording of question 8 leads the answer.
- **9. Instrument feedback.** "Which of my questions were unclear, or felt like they were nudging you toward an answer?" Used to find instrument problems. Treated as a hypothesis about the guide, not as participant evidence.

## Known weaknesses to check in the pilot (written before running)

- Question 8 is hypothetical and its wording ("a tool that knows the posting and your profile") describes the feature and invites a polite yes.
- Question 5 ("what would you have wanted") is open but the prompt "what stopped you" assumes the participant wanted to prepare more.
- Question 6 introduces AI after question 5. A participant who has not heard of AI mock interviews may answer from imagination.
- Question 1 asks three things at once (role, rounds, format).
