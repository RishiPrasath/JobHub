**A8 run 2 report: Recruit Express Pte Ltd (Singapore staffing agency)**

**Bottom line.** Proceed with care. The agency is real and licensed, and no scam or regulatory action against it was found. Employee sentiment is mixed, and the main review sites could not be read. Confidence: medium on legitimacy, low on culture and pay. Before engaging the consultant, verify them by calling the agency on 6732 6006. The firm says it never asks for Singpass details. Ask for written terms, and ask whether any candidate fee applies.
