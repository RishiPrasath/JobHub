## Grab Holdings (A1, run 2): report

**Company and why:** Grab Holdings, the Singapore-headquartered super-app. Assigned. Role under consideration: mid-level software engineering in Singapore.

**Bottom line:** Apply, with care. Confidence is medium. The company is real, profitable and growing, and it pays well. The caution comes from two things: the 2023 layoff is a precedent, and no actual employee reviews could be read; only aggregate ratings from search snippets.

1. **Identity (high).** Grab Holdings Ltd is listed on Nasdaq as GRAB. Headquartered at 3 Media Close, one-north, Singapore. Founded in 2012 as MyTeksi in Malaysia by Anthony Tan and Tan Hooi Ling, public in 2021. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grab_Holdings and https://www.grab.com/sg/press/others/grab-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-raises-full-year-guidance . No ACRA entry for the Singapore operating entity was seen.
2. **Business and size (medium).** Ride-hailing, delivery and financial services in 8 Southeast Asian countries. Record 57 million monthly transacting users in Q3 2026. Headcount is about 11,000 per Wikipedia (2025), or 12,660 per Revelio (June 2026, a third-party estimate); sources disagree. https://www.reveliolabs.com/companies/grab-holdings/employees
3. **Finances (high).** FY2025 was the first full-year net profit, on revenue of $3.37B (+20%). Q3 2026 revenue $1,112M (+27%), adjusted EBITDA $237M, profit for the period only $18M. FY2026 guidance raised to $4.15-4.20B revenue. $500M of buyback remains. Sources: the Q4 2025 and Q3 2026 releases on grab.com/sg/press, and https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/GRAB/6-k-grab-holdings-ltd-current-report-foreign-issuer-d0b9cdff8057.html

## Tool verdict
OpenSERP did the job well for company financials, news, pay data and interview process. Search relevance was good. DuckDuckGo and Bing both worked, with no CAPTCHAs. Calls took 0-24 seconds. 10 searches and 10 extractions, 1 failure (Glassdoor reviews, 502 extract_failed). One more page, the Blind layoff tracker, returned HTTP 200 but only its title. It cannot give employee sentiment from review sites, the weakest area. Search snippets can differ from the page they point to, as with the Levels.fyi median. Ratings: search relevance 4/5, extraction quality 4/5, coverage 3/5, reliability 4/5. Rely on it with caveats: use it to vet the company and prepare, but read reviews for the target team separately.
