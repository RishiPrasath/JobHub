# Low-Fidelity Analyst Walkthrough

Date: 2026-10-27

## Scope and limitation

This is a structured analyst walkthrough using the three captured fixtures and the corrected records. It is not a rendered-interface test and not participant research. The Part-Time Admin Assistant includes the user's recorded decision; the other two do not have a user decision recorded.

## R4-JB-003 — Class 4 Driver

### Evidence surfaced

- Salary range and its overtime/allowance qualifiers
- Shift pattern, six-day week and rest days
- Class 4 licence and additional qualifications
- Pandan Loop and port travel
- Full-time, long-term arrangement
- MyCareersFuture and visible WhatsApp route
- `0% skills matched` as a platform signal

### Uncertainty surfaced

- Role title and legal employer are not visible in the supplied fixture.
- Allowance guarantees and overtime calculation are unknown.
- Response time and vacancy authenticity beyond the captured content are unknown.

### Workflow result

The workflow can explain what is known and what requires verification. It must prevent the monthly income wording from becoming a guaranteed base-salary claim.

### Decision state

Not selected in this analyst walkthrough. The workflow presents questions rather than inferring a user decision.

## R4-JB-006 — Vehicle Mechanic

### Evidence surfaced

- Full-time employment and 5.5-day work pattern
- S$2600–3000 salary
- Automotive qualification and Class 3/4/5 wording
- Singapore-only wording and working-hours evidence
- Updated and expiry dates
- Response-time indicator as a source signal

### Uncertainty surfaced

- Duties are not visible.
- Benefits and assessment stages are unknown.
- Ownership of the response indicator is unknown.
- Expiry does not establish hiring closure.
- Legal employer context is not established.

### Correction exercised

The model's false contradiction was rejected because the no-import instruction was an evaluation rule, not a conflict in the posting.

### Decision state

Not selected in this analyst walkthrough.

## R4-JB-011 — Part-Time Admin Assistant

### Evidence surfaced

- Part-time office-based role
- S$12–S$15 hourly range
- 2–3 weekdays per week and negotiable hours
- Resume-first process with details deferred to interview
- Platform safety and reporting signals
- URL/title/body identity contradiction

### Uncertainty surfaced

- Actual duties are not visible.
- Qualifications are not visible.
- Exact rate determination is not stated.
- Legal employer or recruiter relationship is not established.
- Interview expectations are not stated.
- Related Administration Officer salary teaser must not be treated as this role's compensation.

### Human decision

The user's recorded decision was `decline` because meaningful role, compensation, employer and interview information is missing before resume submission. This is not a legitimacy verdict.

## Cross-posting walkthrough result

The workflow consistently exposes the main evidence categories across different occupations. It also shows why a single fit score would be misleading: the important unknowns differ by role.

The workflow is ready for a low-fidelity interface test, but this walkthrough does not establish usability, comprehension time or accessibility compliance.
