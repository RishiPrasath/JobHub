# User stories (draft, derived from as-is journeys)

Status: draft for owner review; needs only, no solutions. Final storage location is an open decision (see `_drafts/review-deck-plan.md`).

Source: the four as-is journeys (searching, applying, interviewing, offer to first day) in `02_research/03_research-rounds/09_job-seeker-processes-as-is/runs/2026-11-07-how-job-seekers-actually-job-hunt/journeys/`. They rest on desk research, mostly search snippets, and much of it is old, US-based or vendor data. Every story is a need the journeys give evidence for, not a validated finding.

**Update after the gap-fill research (2026-11-07):** confidence ratings below are the first-draft ratings. After the gap-fill run, supported stories rise from 9 to 20 and thin falls from 8 to 3. The updated ratings and reasons are in `02_research/03_research-rounds/09_job-seeker-processes-as-is/runs/2026-11-07-gap-fill/findings.md`. The journeys have not yet been edited to reflect the new evidence.

Evidence tags are copied from the journeys: S = official or primary source, Sn = snippet or secondary write-up, A = anecdote or small sample, I = inference.
Confidence levels:
- **supported:** S, or several Sn.
- **weak:** a single Sn, A or I, or no tag in the journey.
- **thin:** the journey itself says the evidence is thin.

Tags were applied as written. Stories on negotiation and counteroffers rest on several surveys, but the journey marks them dated, US and self-reported, so they are rated weak.

## (a) Cross-cutting needs

1. **Knowing whether a job and employer are genuine and open to me.** Runs from search through to the offer. Stories: S-05, S-06, S-11, S-21, S-35, S-36, S-42, S-43. The best-evidenced theme (S-05 and S-35 are supported).
2. **Knowing where I stand, and not being left in silence.** After applying, after interviews, and while waiting on an offer. Stories: S-19, S-20, S-21, S-27, S-37.
3. **Knowing what to say and ask about money.** Expected pay, current pay, fair benchmarks, negotiation, comparing offers. Stories: S-17, S-32, S-33, S-34, S-44.
4. **Knowing what the process will look like before I commit.** Rounds, formats, scoring, checks, probation. Stories: S-22, S-23, S-25, S-26, S-28, S-40.
5. **Getting a fair look despite weak access or a non-standard path.** Referrals, route to a person, CV readability, explaining a switch. Stories: S-04, S-07, S-13, S-14, S-15, S-24.
6. **Having terms clear and in writing.** Stories: S-30, S-31, S-17, S-40 (supported by S-level sources: TAFEP, MOM).

## (b) Unknown, needs research

Gaps the journeys flag; no stories because there is no evidence.
- How people track a shortlist (1.6) and their applications (2.7).
- Whether job alerts actually mismatch what people want (1.3), which is "not sourced".
- Singapore time to response, current job-search duration, and drop-off by stage.
- Search behaviour of foreign professionals and channels used by low-wage workers in Singapore.
- Whether following up or keeping proof of submission helps (2.5, 2.6).
- Whether the 14-day advertising rule makes some postings not actually open to locals (inference only).
- Differences in interview practice between government, multinational and small employers.
- Singapore negotiation rates, typical pay uplift and counteroffer outcomes.
- Background-check coverage and turnaround (vendor blogs only).
- Stages 4.3, 4.6 and 4.8 (except probation), which carry no evidence tag.
- Low-wage worker and foreign professional experience across all four journeys.
- How candidates experience first-day admin (Singpass, bank, tax, CPF).

## (c) Cautions

Stories on weak or thin evidence should not drive decisions on their own.
- **Thin (the journeys say so):** S-09, S-10, S-12, S-18, S-41, S-42, S-43, S-44 (foreign professionals and low-wage workers).
- **Untagged or guidance only:** S-07, S-15, S-19, S-24, S-26, S-29, S-32, S-33, S-36, S-37.
- **US, dated or vendor data:** S-13, S-14, S-34, S-38, S-28. S-22 leans on a 2017 regional survey plus anecdotes.
- **Inference:** S-06 rests on the researcher's inference that the advertising rule makes some postings not open to locals.
- **Small or convenience samples:** S-04 (n=105 poll) and S-21.
- **General caveat:** the journeys' first pass was done by small-model agents and the lead did not re-open primary sources. Even the "supported" stories (S-01, S-05, S-16, S-20, S-22, S-30, S-31, S-35, S-40) should be checked against primary sources before they guide any decision.
