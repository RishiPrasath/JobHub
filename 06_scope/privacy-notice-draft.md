# Privacy notice (draft for first real users)

Status: draft, not legal advice. Written from [decision 0006](decisions/0006-third-build-scope.md) and [0008](decisions/0008-open-items-resolved.md). Use only after the owner has read it and edited it to match what the app really does.

## What JobHub stores

- Your profile: contact details, work and education entries, skills, languages and the extras you choose to add.
- Your job preferences.
- Jobs you save: the posting text you pasted, our review of it, your notes, your decision, and any documents you made.
- Answers you save for forms (for example "why this role").

We do not ask for or store: NRIC or passport number, date of birth, gender, race, religion, marital status, disability or health, photograph, National Service status, salary history, criminal record, CPF or bank details.

## Why

To review postings, compare them with your profile, help you write your documents, and keep your application records. Nothing is sent to employers. JobHub never applies for you or fills in forms.

## Your resume file

If you upload a resume, we read it, you confirm each field, and then we delete the file. Only the fields you confirmed are kept.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Once a year we ask whether you want to keep, export or delete your data, and give you a grace period to answer. Archived jobs and saved answers are deleted when you delete the job. Deleting your account deletes everything.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it with other users, or use your archived text to improve models without telling you and asking again.

## Notes

Please do not put other people's personal details in your notes.

## Your choices

You can export or delete any job, any saved answer, or your whole account at any time from the app.

## Consent

Before you upload anything, you will be asked to confirm that you have read this notice.
