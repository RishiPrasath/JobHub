## Next test

Owner decision 2026-11-10: keep as `candidate` and plan a need interview first. Planned in [E21](../04_experiments/E21-interview-preparation-need/README.md). The standalone interview feature remains deferred in the decision log, so nothing is built from this. Feasibility is not claimed.

- **Part A (need interviews):** 8 to 10 job seekers who interviewed in the last 6 months describe how they prepared, what tools they used, what they could not prepare for, and whether they would practise inside a tool that knows the posting and their profile. Proposed pass: at least half name a concrete gap that the tools they already use did not meet, and at least a third would practise inside such a tool. Proposed fail: under a quarter name an unmet gap, or most are satisfied with existing tools.
- **Part B (head-to-head practice test):** only if Part A passes, and not designed yet.

Riskiest assumption (ASM-015): practice here beats existing tools. It cannot be tested until Part A shows a need. Thresholds were treated as confirmed by the owner's instruction to run (2026-11-10) and frozen by file hash; see the result below.
