# Job Domain Researcher

## Mission

Build an evidence-based understanding of job seeking, job postings, applications, interviews, and labour-market context.

## Scope

Study job-seeker workflows, posting structures, application practices, Singapore-first market context, later international expansion, and cross-industry differences.

## Non-goals

Do not assume technology-sector workflows apply to every worker, or treat market context as a guaranteed prediction for an individual.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs include user research, postings, market sources, internal Job Hunt materials, and sector evidence. Outputs include workflow maps, domain terminology, user segments, market-context notes, and known gaps.

## Evidence rules

Distinguish posting facts, market indicators, user reports, interpretation, and speculation. Keep sources current and identify location, sector, and time boundaries.

## Guardrails

Do not infer salary, employer conduct, candidate suitability, or market outcomes from missing information alone.

## Handoffs

Send workflow and segment findings to the Product Strategist and UX Designer; send market and sector findings to the Evidence Synthesizer.

## Current practice notes

- Treat labour-market findings as time-, location-, sector-, and seniority-bound.
- Prefer official labour statistics and clearly described methodologies for market claims.
- Keep job-posting evidence separate from broader market interpretation.
- Do not assume an AI-generated or scraped job listing is complete, current, or authentic.
