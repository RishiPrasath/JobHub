What the research suggests

Useful support may reduce uncertainty and repeated effort.

People need help judging opportunities

Actual duties, working conditions and verified experience matter alongside job titles.

Preparation includes checking and recovery

Re-entry, corrections, scheduling and unfinished work add to the writing itself.

Truthful reuse is worth testing

Real examples can support resumes and interview practice without invented achievements.

The simplest useful workflow remains open

Manual documents, peers and career coaching offer meaningful alternatives.

Synthesis of reported mechanisms. Their prevalence and JobHub benefit remain unmeasured.2 / 21

Interview preparation and practice

Guidance recommends explaining real experience and rehearsing aloud.

Preparation material

  • Role and employer research with sources

  • Questions about duties and expectations
  • Interview format and practical arrangements

Practice and return

  • Rehearse aloud with a peer, coach or tool
  • Record what the attempt demonstrated
  • Revisit weak examples without inventing results
  • Resume with one clear next session

Different AI interventions, different outcomes

Primary studies provide useful findings with specific transfer limits.

InterventionReported resultBoundary
Algorithmic resume writing assistanceHigher hiring probability, with no detected employer-satisfaction lossAn online labour-market experiment, not a current chatbot comparison
Generative drafts for employer job postsLess employer writing time and more posts, without a discernible increase in matchesA different intervention in an online labour market
Distinct experiments with overlapping authorship. Neither measures JobHub benefit.13 / 21
Reading notes and sources

C01: arXiv abstract submitted 2023-01-19, algorithmic resume-writing assistance. C02: author-hosted More, but Worse working paper, 2026-09-04 revision, employer job-post generation. C01 reports an 8% relative hiring-probability increase in its setting. C02 reports reduced employer drafting time and no discernible net matching increase; its welfare accounting is model-based. Authors and intervention details overlap in provenance, but these are different experiments, not replications or a provider ranking. No JobHub or Singapore net-effort effect follows.
