# Job-seeker research story: Round 00 evidence audit

Audit date: 2026-10-18, Asia/Singapore. Status: evidence audit and presentation recommendations for review. This document does not accept product scope, a platform, a development method, or research conclusions on the owner's behalf.

## Audit conclusion

The strongest defensible story is about the decisions and constraints people face while seeking work. Some sampled job seekers report uncertainty about expectations and responses. Other studied groups face schedules, care responsibilities, pay or working conditions that application preparation cannot resolve. These problems can overlap, but the evidence does not rank their burden. Reports of assessment effort are useful illustrations with a weaker basis for claims about substantial or avoidable time loss.

AI assistance has evidence of benefit for particular writing interventions, alongside reported checking, editing, selective use and accessibility problems. Existing assistants, career products and human services already cover much of the proposed feature set. Neither a vacant market nor a superior JobHub workflow has been demonstrated. Continuity, truthful evidence reuse and lower correction effort are testable proposals.

The UX/platform and AI SDLC material is strongest as documented constraints, distinctions and experiment designs. It supplies no observed JobHub usability, preferred device, winning platform, model benchmark or local productivity gain. Its proposed participant counts, timing targets, scenarios and quality gates must never be presented as study results.

The promoted reports generally retain these boundaries. Their main weakness for a new presentation is chronology: some next-step language describes the state before later rounds completed. A presentation should explain what Round 00 established, then explicitly hand over to later evidence. It should not repeat the old queue as today's research status.

## Scope, method and confidence language

Read all 43 files under `02_research/03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/` and all nine files under `02_research/04_results/`: seven thematic reports, `00_cross-research-synthesis.md`, and the results README. This includes every Round 00 run brief, note, source index, limitation, finding, specialist handoff and review. Three required instruction/role files and six supporting/context files bring the inspected repository inventory to 61 files. The exact inventory is at the end.

The Evidence Synthesizer role governs classification and provenance here. This auditor read the recorded specialist outputs and reconciled them; it did not rerun their original research sessions, statistical analyses or private-workspace inspection. Historical claims that specialists opened sources, used native agents or ran commands remain claims supported by those run records. They are not newly observed execution by this auditor.

Seven external primary-source pages were reopened for a focused check, listed below. Other external claims inherit the recorded inspection and limitations of their named collector. This is a complete audit of the requested local corpus, with sampled external verification, not a complete replication of every citation. Existing slides were not used as a source or a coverage boundary. No new participant study, product test or implementation experiment was performed.

Use the following labels at claim level:

- **Observed document fact:** a file, passage, documented distinction or tracker state was inspected. This does not mean a person's behaviour was observed.
- **Source-reported empirical finding:** a publisher reports a survey, experiment, interview or administrative outcome. Keep its population, method and dates.
- **Documented offering or guidance:** a vendor, platform, standard or authority describes a service, capability, rule or practice. This is not proof of runtime performance or compliance.
- **Interpretation:** the specialist's or auditor's reconciliation of evidence.
- **Historical proposal:** a suggested product, flow, test, sample, threshold or process that had not been executed in that run.
- **Unknown:** the corpus does not establish the answer.

High confidence means strong support for the narrow statement identified, often a documentary fact or a defined historical measure. Moderate means bounded convergence or a plausible interpretation with material limits. Low/unmeasured means anecdotal support, unresolved measurement or no test. These are qualitative judgments, not calculated probabilities, severity scores or product priorities.

## Sample and evidence limits that must survive compression

Round 00 is exploratory desk research, not a systematic review or representative JobHub survey. The AI run retains 23 external records and seven initial internal process records plus one linked-contract review; friction retains 13 records; remaining research reuses these families and adds workstream registers. These are activity counts, not independent replications. Multiple pages, reused MOM reports, the same publisher and successive reviewer summaries must not be counted as new empirical support.

The samples lean toward English-language graduates, professionals, online freelance work and people reachable through particular communities or programmes. Non-degree pathways, service/trades/operational worker voices, smaller employers, informal hiring, non-English use, low digital confidence, assisted/offline routes, phone-only users and diverse accessibility needs remain thin. Supported-employment cases improve context without representing every lower-income worker or returner. None of the reports establishes the strongest first segment.

Private process documents show intended controls, not adherence, detection accuracy, completion time, saved effort or employment outcomes. Their authenticity phases conflict with some package-map tables. This is a concrete documentation issue, not evidence that the process failed in use. This audit did not reopen original private documents or inspect profiles, conversations, applications or contracts. Their private locators are intentionally not copied into the presentation audit.

No Round 00 researcher directly observed people using JobHub. No controlled six-tool comparison, prototype usability study, cross-device test, model benchmark, payment test, native-agent configuration smoke test or software implementation experiment was completed there. The [synthetic fixture](../../05_evidence/datasets-and-examples/2026-10-18-synthetic-workflow-check.md) was prepared, not executed in that run. Later fictional work does not retroactively turn it into a product benchmark.

Limited/gated access remains material: PeopleScout and Morgan McKinley full reports; DORA full report; PDPC full AI guideline PDFs; OWASP 2026 download; some SkillsFuture/career-service pages; Apple's current web-push documentation. The collectors used accessible summaries or qualified alternatives and recorded failures. An audit of their notes cannot establish the contents of the unread material.
