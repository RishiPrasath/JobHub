# Product and User Research

## Use this instruction for

Understanding job-seeker goals, workflows, segments, constraints, job-posting interpretation, application preparation, and follow-through.

## Assigned team

- **Primary:** Job Domain Researcher for domain and workflow structure.
- **Support:** Job-Seeker Practices Researcher for observed and reported behavior; UX and User-Flow Designer for flow and friction implications.
- **Synthesis:** Evidence Synthesizer.
- **Review:** Product Strategist; add UX Expert when accessibility or usability is central.

## Questions to cover

- How people discover, save, compare, apply to, and follow up on jobs.
- Which moments create time pressure, uncertainty, anxiety, or abandonment.
- How needs differ across career stage, industry, employment status, language, ability, and digital confidence.
- What users want from job-posting analysis, candidate comparison, company research, application preparation, and interview preparation.

## Required output

Produce a workflow map, segment notes, pain points, workarounds, evidence gaps, and testable product implications. Do not convert a stated preference into a general user requirement without qualification.
