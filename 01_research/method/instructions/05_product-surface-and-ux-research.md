# Product Surface and UX Research

## Use this instruction for

Determining how users should move between discovery, capture, analysis, application preparation, interview preparation, and tracking across phones, tablets, laptops, desktops, browsers, and other surfaces.

## Assigned team

- **Primary:** UX and User-Flow Designer.
- **Support:** Job-Seeker Practices Researcher for interruptions and actual behavior; Job Domain Researcher for workflow context.
- **Synthesis:** Evidence Synthesizer.
- **Review:** UX Expert and Product Strategist; add Quality and Evaluation Engineer for usability or accessibility testing.

## Questions to cover

- Where users discover jobs and where they do serious preparation.
- How limited time and context switching affect follow-through.
- Which actions need immediate capture and which require a larger workspace.
- Trade-offs among responsive web, mobile-first, native, progressive web, browser-assisted, and multi-surface options.
- Accessibility, content clarity, error recovery, notifications, offline needs, and device constraints.

## Required output

Produce end-to-end flow maps, surface responsibilities, usability and accessibility risks, prototype questions, and a recommendation only when evidence supports one.
