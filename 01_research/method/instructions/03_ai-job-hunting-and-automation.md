# AI Job Hunting and Automation

## Use this instruction for

Researching how job seekers use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, dedicated career tools, automation services, spreadsheets, notes, and other tools in daily job hunting.

## Assigned team

- **Primary:** Job-Seeker Practices Researcher.
- **Support:** AI Tools Expert for capability and trade-off comparison; Job Domain Researcher for job-search context.
- **Synthesis:** Evidence Synthesizer.
- **Review:** Responsible AI Reviewer; add UX Expert for human-control or comprehension issues.

## Questions to cover

- What users automate, what they assist, and what they retain personally.
- How users search, filter, capture, tailor resumes, write cover letters, prepare interviews, and track applications.
- What they verify, edit, submit unchanged, or abandon.
- Trade-offs involving speed, relevance, authenticity, privacy, cost, employer expectations, and application volume.
- Gaps such as generic output, fabricated claims, weak candidate context, poor continuity, and decision fatigue.

## Required output

Produce behavior patterns, tool/workflow comparisons, verification habits, failure modes, unmet needs, and design implications. Separate vendor claims from observed behavior.
