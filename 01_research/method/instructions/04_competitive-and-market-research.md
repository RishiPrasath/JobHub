# Competitive and Market Research

## Use this instruction for

Understanding job boards, application trackers, CV and interview tools, career-coaching products, AI job-search products, market context, pricing, and positioning.

## Assigned team

- **Primary:** Competitive Landscape Researcher.
- **Support:** Job Domain Researcher for market and sector context; AI Tools Expert for AI capability and workflow comparison.
- **Synthesis:** Evidence Synthesizer.
- **Review:** Product Strategist; add Responsible AI Reviewer for privacy or automation claims.

## Questions to cover

- Which audiences and workflows competitors serve.
- What is directly observable versus merely claimed in marketing.
- Where products are strong, weak, crowded, or differentiated.
- Whether gaps are durable user problems or temporary feature gaps.
- Pricing, access, privacy signals, platform limitations, and cross-industry support.

## Required output

Produce a dated comparison matrix, positioning implications, uncertainty notes, naming-confusion risks, and questions requiring user validation. Never copy a competitor feature list into scope without evidence of user value.
