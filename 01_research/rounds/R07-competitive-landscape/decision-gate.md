## Market-gap claim: unvalidated

The [synthesis](round-synthesis.md) says the market is weaker at preserving evidence, explaining uncertainty, representing cross-industry context, verifying employer and role information, and supporting Singapore-specific job seekers. It also describes a practical opening for a Singapore-first product, and says that opening "must be validated rather than assumed".

Treat this as an unvalidated hypothesis:

- It rests on public documentation. No competitor account, paid plan, extension or authenticated workflow was tested ([limitations](limitations.md)).
- Missing public evidence of a capability is not evidence that the capability is absent.
- Singapore support was confirmed most clearly for MyCareersFuture and Jobstreet, so local competition exists.
- The round did not test whether job seekers want or would pay for these gaps to be closed.

## Close, continue or report partial coverage

Report this round as partial coverage. It describes publicly documented workflows and dated pricing. It does not establish that match scores predict outcomes: no independent causal evidence was found that ATS scores, AI tailoring or automatic application volume improves hiring outcomes.

Public pricing and availability are dated and change quickly, so re-check them before reuse. Known contradictions, including Jobright's automation and privacy statements and Careerflow's pricing, remain open in the [limitations](limitations.md).
