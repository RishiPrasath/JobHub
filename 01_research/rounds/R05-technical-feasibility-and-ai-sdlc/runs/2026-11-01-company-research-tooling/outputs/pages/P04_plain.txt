Grab’s Contribution to Singapore Grab’s Economic Impact in Singapore Grab’s ride-hailing and delivery services contributed S$5.2 billion to Singapore’s economy in 2023. The impact supported 117,000 earning opportunities, and generated S$2.5 billion in household income through in-app purchases and the economic activity it supports among the local supply chains in the wider Singapore economy. One of them was Komsan, an assistant chef in a luxury hotel based in the Srinakarin area. As the number of tourists at the hotel plunged, he decided to sign up as a GrabFood delivery-partner to earn an alternative income. Soon after, the hotel ceased operations. Komsan has viewed this change through an optimistic lens, calling it the perfect opportunity for him to embark on a fresh journey after his previous job. Aside from GrabFood deliveries, he now also picks up GrabExpress jobs. It can get tiring, having to shuttle between different locations, but Komsan finds it exciting. And mostly, he’s glad to get his income back on track.
