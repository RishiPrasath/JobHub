# Run: how job seekers actually job-hunt

Date: 2026-11-07 (Singapore). Round: [09](../../README.md). Status: first pass complete.

Desk research, not an experiment. Four Haiku-model agents each researched one process from the job seeker's side (search, apply, interview, offer to first day), told to describe only current practice and never a product. The lead researcher merged the four notes.

## Headline
The job hunt is a loop, not a line: set criteria, search through a few channels, screen postings (including for scams), apply through one of four routes, wait through mostly silence, go through two to five interview and assessment steps, receive and check an offer, and resign and start. Silence after applying and after interviews is the most consistent pain point. Evidence for Singapore specifically is thin and often old; much of the quantified data is from the United States or from vendor surveys.
