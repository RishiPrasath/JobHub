## Disagreements between sources
- Search time per day: the 48-minute American Time Use Survey figure and the 70, 77 and 81 minute survey figures differ; the papers attribute this to design (time diary versus recall) and sample. The weekly-recall measure in the 2011 US study gives about 100 minutes versus 70 by time diary.
- Follow-up: employer surveys from staffing firms (2011, 2017) say employers want follow-up. The Robert Half 2017 page says 100% of HR managers, while the search snippet described a "small sample" for the same release; the page says 300+. No outcome evidence either way.
- Applications per offer: figures run from about 15 to about 300; from vendors with undocumented methods and not comparable across fields.
- Hays 2018 mobile figures: the snippet and the page matched on alerts at 16%. Other markets' figures were not re-read.
