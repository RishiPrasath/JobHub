## Disagreements between sources
- Job scam trend: the SPF H1 2025 figure of 2,701 cases (down 53.1%) sits oddly beside a secondary source's full-year 5,575 cases (H2 would exceed H1). Possible, but the secondary figure is unverified and the two may count differently.
- Fee level versus the cap: MOM's cap is 2 months' salary; HOME and TWC2 report S$13,000 to 18,000 for first-time workers; the TWC2 Burmese survey found an average of about S$3,100 to 3,700. Differences by group, sector and whether fees are paid at home or in Singapore.
- Security floors: one search result gave inconsistent figures; MOM's page resolves it (outsourced basic pay, in-house gross pay).
- TWC2 Burmese survey internal labels: the PDF labels $3,733 as the "average" and $3,100 as the "mean", which cannot both be right.
- Local fee rule: one non-official site claimed a separate local-worker fee rule (one month for jobs paying S$3,500 or less); no MOM support found.
