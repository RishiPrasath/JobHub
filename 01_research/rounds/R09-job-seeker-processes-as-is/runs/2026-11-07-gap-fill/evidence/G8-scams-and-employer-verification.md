## Disagreements between sources
- Overall 2025 case count: news reports (Malay Mail, The Star) give 37,308 scam cases and "more than 50,000" for 2024; the SPF annual brief gives 41,974 scam and cybercrime cases against 55,810. Scams-only versus scams plus cybercrime. Use the SPF brief and say which basis.
- 2025 loss change: snippets said losses fell 17.9% in one place and 19% in another, from S$1.11B to S$913.1M; the PDF text not checked. The H1 2026 brief separately states a 17.9% fall in losses, so one source may have mixed the periods.
- H1 2026 headline: the SPF brief says cases fell 14.4% and losses fell 17.9%; a Bernama headline reported a 17.9% fall in cases and cybercrime combined.
- Job-scam rank: 2025 third by cases and third by losses; H1 2026 fourth by losses; 2024 second on both.
- Willingness to pay for protection: Reeracoen article says one in five; another summary gave 12.8%; unresolved.
- Prevalence of suspicious offers: Reeracoen says 35% met scams in the past year; Indeed says 72% ever met a fake or suspicious opportunity. Different questions; do not compare.
- Page dates: the ACRA page shows both "30 Jan 2026" and a later footer date; the Outsource Accelerator article shows posting May 2025 and "updated" Oct 2026. The earlier date was treated as publication.
