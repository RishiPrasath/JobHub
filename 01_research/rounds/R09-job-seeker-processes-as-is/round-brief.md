# Round 09 brief — How job seekers actually job-hunt (as-is processes)

Date started: 2026-11-07

## Decision or uncertainty

Before designing anything, we need an accurate picture of the processes job seekers follow today: what they do, in what order, what information and documents they need, what they decide, and where it goes wrong. This round records the baseline only.

## Research question

What are the real, current processes people follow when (1) searching for jobs, (2) applying, (3) going through interviews and assessments, and (4) moving from offer to first day? How do they differ by type of person (fresh graduate, mid-career switcher, experienced professional, foreign professional, low-wage worker)?

## Scope

- Current practice as reported by surveys, government sources, university career services and credible guidance.
- General practice, with Singapore as the local context.
- Written from the job seeker's point of view.

## Non-goals

- No product ideas, features, tools or solutions. Nothing here refers to JobHub's ideas.
- No user stories yet. Those come later, derived from this baseline.
- Not primary research with job seekers. Desk research only.

## Stopping rule

Four independent workstreams (search, apply, interview, offer-to-start) each return a sourced note; the lead researcher merges them into one findings document and records what could not be verified. Stop there.
