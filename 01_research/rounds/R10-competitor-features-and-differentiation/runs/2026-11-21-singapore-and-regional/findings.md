# Run E findings: Singapore and Southeast Asia platforms and career tools

Run date: 2026-11-21. Desk research only: no account creation, logins, purchases or form submissions. Pages were read in the owner's existing browser via Kimi WebBridge (session `jobhub-r10-sg`, read-only), plus WebSearch and WebFetch. Personal details visible in logged-in views were deliberately not recorded. FastJobs and GrabJobs showed a Cloudflare bot check, which was not bypassed.

Note: the research subagent was not allowed to write files, so this file was saved by the round lead from its returned report.

Evidence labels as in the [round brief](../../round-brief.md). R07 already covered MyCareersFuture, Jobstreet and LinkedIn; only new AI, safety and URL details are added here.

## 1. MyCareersFuture (MCF) and CareersFinder

- **Users and access:** Singapore Citizens and PRs. Browsing needs no login; applying needs Singpass ("You'll need to log in with Singpass to verify your identity"). (V-site)
- **Matching:** every search card shows "N% skills matched" (7% to 92% today); "Add Skills" control; "Recommended based on your skills & job applications"; describes itself as "AI-powered job search". (V-site)
- **Salary:** every listing showed a monthly range (for example "$8,000 to $16,000 Monthly"); "Minimum Salary" filter. (V-site)
- **Responsiveness:** "TYPICALLY REPLIES IN 30 DAYS" next to employer names. (V-site) MOM (14 Oct 2025) said it is "introducing filters to help jobseekers identify more responsive employers", cross-checks reported vacancies against MCF postings, and that posting the same job on several portals is normal. (V-site, MOM written answer)
- **Job page signals (V-site):** Job ID (format MCF-2026-2061066); posted and closing dates; number of applications; "Report issue"; extracted skills; recommended MySkillsFuture courses; BETA machine translation into Chinese, Malay and Tamil (Microsoft Translator).
- **Scam banner:** officials never ask you to transfer money; ScamShield helpline 1799. (V-site)
- **Scheme filters (V-site, llms.txt):** Career Conversion Programmes; Mid-Career Pathways (40+, allowance up to $3,800/month); GRIT traineeships; flexible work arrangements.
- **CareersFinder:** transferable skills, job outlook, salary data, upskilling suggestions; personalisation needs Singpass. (V-site)
- **Price:** free.
- **Learn:** MCF sets the local bar for honesty signals (salary always shown, closing date, application count, responsiveness, report link). JobHub's posting review should quote these when present and note when a non-MCF posting lacks them.
- **Avoid:** "% skills matched" is a fit score; low percentages on relevant roles show how keyword scores mislead.

## 2. Government and union AI tools

- **Career Kaki (GovTech):** free "AI career coach and AI job search tool"; draws on MCF jobs, MySkillsFuture courses and the Careers & Skills Passport (CSP); Singpass unlocks personalisation; matches to "government-verified vacancies"; salary comparisons. (V-site, page updated 15 Sep 2026) Launched July 2024. (2nd)
- **ResumeBoostSG:** AI resume evaluation "with human expertise", beta, Singpass login. (2nd; page returned 404 today)
- **NTUC AI Career Coach:** AI chat, ATS resume builder, mock video interview with transcript feedback, RIASEC career suggestions; matches to MCF listings graded "stronger / medium / lower match"; Singpass; aimed at mid-career, switchers and retrenched workers. (V-3p, NTUC article 27 Feb 2026, partly promotional)
- **Learn:** free Singpass-backed AI coaches exist, so JobHub must compete on trust, multi-board coverage and claim safety, not on generation.
- **Avoid:** match bands, which are softer verdicts.

## 3. Careers & Skills Passport and SEEK Pass

- MOM, SSG and Jobstreet announced SEEK Pass on 11 Feb 2025: candidates verify and share work credentials using the CSP (employment history at launch) and Accredify (university qualifications). CSP rolled out Nov 2024. (V-site, MOM)
- CSP reportedly integrated with Jobstreet and FastJobs. (2nd)
- **Implication:** verified candidate facts could later feed JobHub's profile and support "never invent claims". Not v1.

## 4. Jobstreet (new items only)

- **Personalised signals while logged in (V-site):** "Very strong applicant" / "Strong applicant"; "How you match: 6 skills and credentials match your profile"; "Salary match" shown even when salary is hidden; "Medium application volume".
- **Employer questions before applying:** expected monthly salary, right to work in Singapore, notice period. (V-site)
- **Company identity:** "Company information Registration No." (UEN) and a company rating (2.8 on the sample job). (V-site)
- **Scam safety:** each job page ends with "Be careful: Don't provide your bank or credit card details when applying for jobs" and "Report this job ad". Security Hub lists current scams: fake overseas processing fees (19 Dec 2025), fake cheque money mule (20 Oct 2025). Trust & Safety "verifies new hirer accounts and reviews job ads flagged by our systems before they are posted". (V-site)
- **Salary display:** many cards show no salary. (V-site)
- **AI history:** AI fit scoring, recommendations and an "approachability" signal since Dec 2023 (V-3p); April 2025 free unlimited employer ads, AI recruiting service and SEEK Pass (2nd); seekAi for employers in the Philippines, Sept 2026 (2nd).
- **Learn:** the UEN on the job page is a strong identity anchor. When salary is hidden, JobHub should say "salary not disclosed".
- **Avoid:** applicant-strength badges.

## 5. LinkedIn (new items only)

- **AI search:** results page shows "How AI search works" and "How promoted jobs are ranked"; `/jobs/search/` redirects to `/jobs/search-results/` with `infoNotice=ai-job-search`. (V-site) Singapore an early 2025 market. (2nd)
- **Verified filter and badge:** "Verified" filter on search (V-site). Badge = verified company page plus a poster who confirmed they work there; a missing badge "does not necessarily mean that the information is incorrect or fraudulent"; cannot be bought; pilot. (V-site, help article)
- **Other signals:** "Actively reviewing applicants", "Be an early applicant", "Under 10 applicants" filter, Premium upsell under AI search. (V-site)
- **Implication:** treat the badge as one input and repeat LinkedIn's own caveat.

## 6. NodeFlair

- **Users:** tech talent in Asia. (V-site)
- **Jobs:** "Aggregated job listings from popular job sites and career pages"; filters for seniority, title, tech stack, company, country, salary; alerts. (V-site) Results did not load today, so card contents unverified. (I)
- **Salaries and reviews:** user-contributed salaries and reviews; annual salary reports (V-site). Press cites a 13 to 25% pay premium for AI skills (2nd). See run D for review provenance issues.
- **Free AI tools (V-site):** ATS Resume Checker (upload resume, paste JD, get score); Resume Builder; AI Interview Prep; Resume Writing Assistant ("rewrite your work experiences ... to job descriptions"); cover letter, email and LinkedIn message writers; salary estimator; One-Click Job Apply Chrome extension ("Start applying to 10,000+ jobs").
- **Claim to note:** repeats "up to 75% of resumes never make it past an ATS screening", unsourced. (V-site)
- **Learn:** closest local toolkit analogue to JobHub: separate generators plus a score plus mass apply, in contrast with JobHub's per-job, verified, decide-first approach.
- **Avoid:** ATS scores, fear statistics, AI rewriting of experience without a claims check.

## 7. Glints (Glints TapJobs)

- **Salary:** often "Not Disclosed". Unflagged data errors (V-site; "not flagged" is I): a full-time Full Stack Developer at "$200 - 300/Month"; a Singapore-located listing priced "Rp 145-160Mio".
- **Employer signals (V-site):** paid "Premium Employer" tag (not verification); "This job post is managed by [recruiter] Online 5 days ago"; company size and office address. Company profiles show a "Verified" status (2nd).
- **Scam safety:** "Glints Safety Tips: Legitimate employers won't ask for contact Telegram or any kind of top-ups or payment", plus "Report This Job". (V-site)
- **AI:** employer-side only; no job-seeker AI found. (2nd / I)
- **Learn:** implausible pay and wrong currency are concrete, quotable review checks.
- **Avoid:** paid badges that look like trust badges.

## 8. Indeed SG

- **Salary:** mixed units; "$14,000 - $16,000 a year" on a Staff/Senior Golang role, very likely a monthly figure entered as yearly. (V-site; interpretation I)
- **Features:** "Easily apply", "View similar jobs with this employer", email alerts, filters for pay, remote, job type, date posted. (V-site)
- **Implication:** when units look wrong, quote and note it; never convert silently.

## 9. Careers@Gov

- `jobs.careers.gov.sg` showed 2,104 results today; "Easy Apply" on many jobs; closing dates shown; no salary on cards; scam banner. (V-site)
- PSD reported in 2022 a scam site reposting Public Service jobs; official advice is to apply only via Careers@Gov or the agency's own site and check for .gov.sg. (V-site)
- **Implication:** "looks like a government job but the URL is not .gov.sg" is a cheap, high-value red flag.

## 10. Lighter coverage

- **FastJobs and GrabJobs:** Cloudflare check, not bypassed (I). FastJobs has in-app chat ("FastChat") and a resume builder (2nd, old press). GrabJobs replaces "Apply" with "Interview Now" via the AIRA chatbot, allows applying without a CV, mentions "Auto-Apply with AI" (JobCopilot) (2nd).
- **eFinancialCareers SG:** salary mostly "Competitive" or "Negotiable". (V-site)
- **Foundit SG:** many salary ranges; "Early Applicant" tags; prompt to "apply to multiple jobs in one go". (V-site)
- **Not covered in depth:** Kalibrr, Hiredly (looks Malaysia-led), Tech in Asia Jobs. (I)

## Singapore context JobHub must respect

1. **Salary rules for Employment Pass jobs (V-site, MOM EP page):** the MCF ad must show the salary to all candidates; max at most twice the min; range must include the salary offered; ad runs 14 consecutive days, restarted by changes to salary, occupation, UEN or vacancies; no offer during the 14 days. Exemptions include employers under 10 staff and jobs paying $22,500/month or more. S Pass has the same twice-the-minimum rule (2nd). No general legal duty to show salary on every ad was found (I).
2. **Tripartite Guidelines (TAFEP), wording to avoid (V-site):** age ("Minimum aged 21", "Young/Youthful working environment"); gender ("Preferably female"); race ("Chinese/Malay/Indian only"); religion; language without reason ("Native Mandarin speaking"); nationality ("EP/S Pass/WP holders preferred"); marital or family status.
3. **Workplace Fairness Act:** second bill passed 4 Nov 2025; Act expected around end-2027 by Gazette notice; employers under 25 staff expected to be exempt at first (2nd). Present findings as guideline concerns, not legal violations.
4. **Scams:**
   - 2025: 5,575 job scam cases, $123.5M lost, third most common scam type (2nd, SPF annual brief summary).
   - Task scams with growing deposits: 215+ cases and $10.6M+ lost since Oct 2025 (V-site, SPF 24 Nov 2025).
   - WhatsApp and Telegram recruitment with cash handovers (2nd, SPF Aug 2025).
   - Fake agency staff (V-site, SPF/GovTech 15 May 2026): quote a real agency licence number, ask victims to change Singpass email and phone, claim a "Certificate of Clearance" is needed. SPF: a COC "is not required for local employment purposes".
   - Fake overseas processing fees and cheque mule scams (V-site, Jobstreet).
   - Standard advice: ScamShield app and 1799.
5. **Agency check:** MOM's Employment Agencies Directory lists licensed agencies and personnel (2nd). A quoted licence number alone is not proof (V-site, SPF).
6. **Singpass:** JobHub must never ask for Singpass details, and should flag postings that do.

## Patterns

- **Fit scores are everywhere:** MCF %, Jobstreet applicant strength, NTUC match bands, NodeFlair ATS score.
- **Safety means a static banner and a report link.** No board reads the specific posting.
- **Salary transparency is uneven.** MCF always shows it; Glints, Jobstreet, eFinancialCareers and Careers@Gov often hide it. Data errors pass through unflagged.
- **Free AI resume and coaching tools are the norm** (government, NTUC, NodeFlair).
- **Verification is moving to credentials** (CSP, SEEK Pass, LinkedIn badges), but none checks whether a posting is honest.
- **Mass apply is being pushed:** NodeFlair, GrabJobs, Foundit, Easy Apply.

## Table stakes users expect

1. A visible salary range, or a clear "not disclosed" note, plus a salary filter.
2. Freshness signals: posted date, closing date, applicant count or employer response speed.
3. A scam warning and a "report this job" link.
4. Company basics on the posting: UEN, size, address, rating.
5. Free resume help and job alerts.
6. Mobile search with filters for work arrangement, job type, date and salary.

## Gaps JobHub could own

1. **Posting-specific scam and fairness review that quotes the risky lines.** Every board has only a generic banner.
2. **Employer identity check across sources, each with its limits stated:** UEN, .gov.sg check for government jobs, a pointer to MOM's agency directory, LinkedIn's badge (with LinkedIn's own caveat).
3. **Salary sanity notes:** Glints "$200 - 300/Month" full-time and a rupiah-priced Singapore listing; Indeed "$14,000 - $16,000 a year"; Jobstreet "Salary match" with salary hidden.
4. **Quoted evidence instead of fit scores.** Not scoring is unusual locally.
5. **Claim-safe, human-verified documents stored per job, across boards.** Free AI rewriting is common, but none checks claims; mass apply is being pushed.
6. **A neutral workspace across boards** (MCF, Jobstreet, LinkedIn, Careers@Gov, niche boards), built on the search-link builder and paste intake without crawling.

## Limitations

- Personalised logged-in views may differ from a logged-out user's.
- FastJobs and GrabJobs not read directly; NodeFlair job results did not load.
- 2025 scam totals come from a search summary, not the SPF PDF. WFA timing comes from law-firm commentary. Jobstreet and LinkedIn filter codes marked I are inferred.
- No new Singapore consumer AI job-search startup from 2025 or 2026 found beyond government and NTUC tools; FUKU.AI is employer-side.
- Sentiment is anecdotal (press reports of social posts about ghost jobs and hidden salaries). Reddit not read directly.
