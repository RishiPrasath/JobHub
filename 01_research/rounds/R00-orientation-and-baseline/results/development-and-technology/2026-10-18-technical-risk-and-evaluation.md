# Technical options, responsible AI and evaluation

Date: 2026-10-18. Reviewed proposals; no stack, provider, retention or launch approval.

The [technical report](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-remaining-research/technical-and-responsible-ai.md) compares capture routes and local/cloud/hybrid operation. Its proposed source/claim model preserves provenance, completeness, versions, user confirmation and correction. Null fields retain reasons; capture failure is not employer nondisclosure.

Capture rights must be assessed for the particular source and operation. Privacy and provider terms depend on the actual product, feature, account and data route. A policy statement or a partial guideline review cannot certify compliance. Local storage with a remote model still transfers information.

Retention must distinguish raw originals, permitted evidence excerpts/crops and derived claims. Delete according to the approved contract and show unavailable evidence when citations can no longer be verified. “Immutable” versions are not exempt from deletion. No numerical retention proposal has been adopted.

The report maps injection, unauthorized action, rendering, leakage, supply-chain and fetch/consumption risks to concrete fixtures. Controls require application-level enforcement and tests, not instructions to the model alone. Age/provider eligibility is an explicit pilot prerequisite. These are plausible-risk controls, not findings from a code security scan.

Compare tools on identical tasks against deterministic/manual baselines; score evidence, uncertainty, usefulness and correction burden. Measure actual total cost and latency, failures and retries. Keep consequential failures separate from average scores. No model winner or operating estimate is supported without executing the comparison.

Passing future finite tests will not itself establish universal safety or fairness.
