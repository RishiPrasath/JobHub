# AI-assisted job hunting: first research result

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: specialist-reviewed exploratory synthesis with coordinator corrections; pending human acceptance. No product scope, platform, stack, or automation decision adopted.

## Recommendation for discussion

Test a small workflow: **save one role → understand its evidence and unknowns → return after an interruption → choose the next action**. Then compare truthful application drafting and interview rehearsal as separate extensions. This prioritization is the coordinator's research proposal, not a demonstrated market preference.

## What the evidence supports

- There is bounded evidence for particular writing interventions, but it does not establish a universal benefit from generative drafting or automated applications. [Empirical qualifications](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-ai-job-hunting-workflows/findings.md)
- Reported workflows combine AI with editing, saved material, and ordinary tracking tools; these reports do not estimate population behavior. [Behavior research](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-ai-job-hunting-workflows/specialists/job-seeker-practices.md)
- Competing products already offer overlapping career capabilities. Reducing context loss and checking effort is a differentiation hypothesis requiring direct comparison. [Six-tool matrix](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-ai-job-hunting-workflows/specialists/ai-tools.md)
- Singapore evidence motivates cross-sector research but does not settle device preference or prove hiring benefits. [Local evidence](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-ai-job-hunting-workflows/specialists/job-domain.md)
- The internal process documents offer concrete examples of truthful claims, source authenticity, and application-state distinctions. They are a personal case, not measured product validation. [Sanitized case summary](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-ai-job-hunting-workflows/specialists/internal-workflow.md)

## What remains open

Mobile versus web; the strongest initial segment; whether continuity is a paid or recurring need; real correction/time savings; current tool output quality; sourcing/networking effectiveness; and usability across industries, languages, and accessibility needs.

No firsthand interviews, live product comparisons, or prototype trials were performed. A synthetic test fixture is prepared but unexecuted. See [limitations](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-ai-job-hunting-workflows/limitations.md).

Broader product research and AI-era development research remain in the backlog.

## Next useful evidence

Observe a small, diverse set of job seekers completing realistic tasks and compare those tasks with their existing tools. Measure comprehension, unsupported claims, correction effort, recovery, and desire to return. Keep individual access failures visible. Use consented participant research and synthetic data for product comparisons; do not upload private career materials by default.

Any eventual decision should link this result in the strategy decision log. Research execution approval does not itself accept the proposed product direction.
