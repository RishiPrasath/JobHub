# Round 00 brief — orientation and baseline

## Decision context

What is already known from the completed desk research, what remains uncertain, and which research activity should happen next?

## Scope

This round consolidates the prior 2026-10-18 desk-research activities. It includes public research, internal process material where it was explicitly scoped, product and UX desk research, development-method research, technical and responsible-AI research, and Codex delivery research.

## Exclusions

The material in this round must not be represented as direct user validation, representative prevalence, product-market fit, usability testing, live tool benchmarking, or an accepted product decision.

## Completion rule

The round is complete enough when prior evidence is traceable, contradictions and limitations are visible, and the next bounded research activity has an explicit brief and stopping rule.

## Outcome

The round recommends a public-web user-insight activity followed by a synthetic workflow or low-fidelity flow comparison. Product scope, platform, stack, model/provider, automation level, pricing, and final decisions remain open.
