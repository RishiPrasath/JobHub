## Interpretation rules

Official capability documentation establishes a documented offering, not that it worked for JobHub. Standards and regulator guidance establish obligations/principles within their scope, not compliance of an unbuilt product. Vendor playbooks and marketing are interested sources. Empirical research retains its population, task and date limits. Public anecdotes and the owner's internal process can suggest hypotheses but not prevalence. A blocked or partial page cannot support an unseen claim.

Keep personal evidence out of public source registers. Do not republish full articles, private workflows or third-party postings merely to make this repository self-contained. Recheck volatile capabilities/prices/provider policies at tool selection, legislation/terms at actual use, and naming records before launch.
