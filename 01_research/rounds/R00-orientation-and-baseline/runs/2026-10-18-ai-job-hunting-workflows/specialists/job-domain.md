# Job Domain Researcher handoff

Specialist: Boyle. Access date: 2026-10-18. Read-only desk research; all retained sources opened. No interviews or product trials.

## Source records and findings

- D01: [MOM Job Vacancies 2025](https://stats.mom.gov.sg/iMAS_PdfLibrary/mrsd_JV2025.pdf), published 2026-03-20. Employer survey referencing 2025-09-30, fieldwork September-December 2025; 16,910 establishments responded, 85.9% response rate; private establishments with at least 25 employees plus public sector. Qualifications were not the main determinant for 79.6% of vacancies, but skills and experience still mattered. Non-PMET vacancies span service, manual, transport, and security work. High confidence within coverage; smaller private employers excluded. This does not remove occupation-specific credential requirements.
- D02: [MOM Labour Force 2025 findings](https://stats.mom.gov.sg/iMAS_Tables1/LabourForce/LabourForce_2025/mrsd_2025LabourForce_survey_findings.pdf), published 2026-01-29. Full-time residents averaged 43.8 working hours per week; caregiving kept 72,500 residents outside the labour force. These describe constraints, not job-search time or device preference. [Methodology](https://stats.mom.gov.sg/iMAS_Tables1/LabourForce/LabourForce_2025/mrsd_2025LabourForce_coverage_methodology.pdf): stratified sample of 33,000 dwellings, 27,324 responding households, 86.8% response rate; fieldwork March-July 2025. Dormitories, worksite accommodation, and cross-border commuters excluded.
- D03: [MOM/WSG Career Health factsheet](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/budget2025/cos-2025-factsheet-on-career-health.pdf), COS 2025, exact day unstated. CareersFinder uses skills adjacency/job-transition data for occupations, training, and jobs. Administrative figures report over 55,000 users by December 2024 and applications across more occupations after use. No comparison group or hiring-outcome evaluation provided: greater exploration is not demonstrated matching or placement improvement.
- D04: [NTUC AI Career Coach walkthrough](https://www.ntuc.org.sg/ucarecentre/news/I-tested-the-NTUC-AI-Career-Coach-does-it-work/), 2026-02-27. One named author describes goals, resume recommendations, revisions, and six-question video interview practice with transcript feedback. First-person report published by the service's organization; not an independent evaluation or a test by this research team. Singpass/quiet-space requirements matter to access; representative usability and outcomes unknown.
- D05: [Singapore Global Network, AI and hiring interview](https://singaporeglobalnetwork.gov.sg/stories/business/ai-is-changing-hiring-heres-what-employers-are-looking-for-now/), 2026-10-02. Expert interview stresses authentic impact and ability to explain reasoning. No sampled employer population; professional hiring emphasis limits transfer. Practitioner guidance, not employer consensus.
- D06: [Hays Singapore AI in 2024](https://www.hays.com.sg/press-release/content/ai-in-2024), 2024-01-22, outside requested window. Reports 49.4% application-related AI use but conflicts on sample: subtitle 1,175 professionals versus body 175 professionals and 412 employers. Fieldwork/recruitment/weighting undisclosed. Historical context only; not a national or current adoption estimate.
- D07: [Morgan McKinley 2026 Global AI Report summary](https://www.morganmckinley.com/uk/resource/our-2026-global-ai-report-8-key-workplace-ai-trends), 2026, exact publication day unavailable. Q3 survey of 1,962 employees and 144 employers in seven markets. Singapore subgroup denominator/weighting absent; inconsistent training figures within summary. Directional recruitment-industry source; no national inference or trend calculation. Full report form-gated and not obtained.

## Interpretation and disagreements

Local evidence supports studying truthful skill representation, adjacent occupations, preparation, and interviews. NTUC's ATS emphasis and SGN's human/context emphasis differ; neither establishes a universal screening process. Avoid promising that keyword optimization will secure review.

Different survey populations, dates, and AI-use definitions cannot be combined into an adoption trend. Employer use of parsing is not evidence of acceptance of AI-written applications.

No adequate local evidence found here establishes mobile preference, interrupted-session frequency, or completion by occupation. Working hours/caregiving motivate tests but do not prove a particular interface is needed. A technical professional's CV workflow should not stand in for all frontline, licensed, or shift work.
