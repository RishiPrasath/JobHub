# AI-assisted job-hunting workflows: research brief

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: approved for research; conclusions require human acceptance.

## Objective

Understand how job seekers use AI, what they retain under personal control, and where work breaks down. Compare candidate first workflows for JobHub without committing product scope, platform, or architecture.

## Scope and method

- Singapore-first, cross-industry; label international evidence and transfer limitations.
- Prioritize evidence published between 2024-10-18 and 2026-10-18. Label older foundational evidence explicitly.
- Desk research: original studies, transparent surveys, public first-person accounts, and official tool documentation. Separate reported use from demonstrated capability and causal outcomes.
- Read the user's internal Job Hunt workspace and directly relevant linked workflow/agent documents. Summarize process patterns only; do not copy personal profiles, applications, credentials, or contact details into this Git repository or external search queries.
- Controlled tool comparisons use synthetic inputs only where accessible without purchases, new accounts, external submissions, or private-data upload. Record unperformed tests as unperformed.
- Interviews and participant observation are follow-up proposals, not activities performed in this run.

## Assignment

The coordinator handles the Research Planner role, internal evidence, integration, and final files. Parallel read-only specialists: Job-Seeker Practices Researcher (actual behavior and studies), AI Tools Expert (capabilities and dedicated competitors), Job Domain Researcher (Singapore and cross-industry evidence). UX interpretation follows internal/external collection. Evidence Synthesizer then checks provenance and contradictions; Responsible AI Reviewer reviews the resulting findings. Review is sequential where dependent.

## Outputs and boundaries

Record sources, notes, specialist handoffs, findings, limitations, and a reviewed thematic result. Product recommendations remain proposals. No applications, employer contact, purchases, commits, pushes, deployment, or product implementation.

## Stopping rule

Finish the initial scan when each major workflow area has evidence or an explicit gap, material conclusions are traceable, contradictions and transfer limits are visible, and candidate workflows can be compared. Do not force a minimum number of positive findings or extend desk research indefinitely to simulate user validation. Identify questions requiring interviews or prototype testing.
