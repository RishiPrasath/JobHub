# Limitations

## Sampling

This was a bounded convenience sample chosen to compare page ecosystems and contexts. It is not representative of all Singapore job boards, employers, occupations or industries. Seven of the 20 page observations were substantive vacancy detail pages; the remaining pages were search, landing, support, blocked, maintenance or partial pages.

## Time and volatility

Job pages can change, expire, redirect or disappear. The records are dated observations from 2026-10-19. A later reader should not assume that the displayed vacancy remains current.

## Access and rendering

- LinkedIn detail content did not render in the inspected Chrome view.
- Some pages may contain expandable, delayed or personalized content that was not loaded or opened.
- No login, CAPTCHA bypass, application interaction or external redirect was used.
- “Not visible” therefore describes the observed access state, not the complete underlying page.

## Content and ownership

The display platform, employer, recruiter, agency, government body and client can be different parties. Ownership classifications are research classifications from visible context, not verified contractual authorship.

## Inference limits

The inventory cannot establish:

- whether an employer is genuinely hiring;
- whether a posting is fake, ghost or deceptive;
- whether a badge, response indicator or applicant count is accurate;
- whether a salary is guaranteed or comparable beyond the wording shown;
- whether a missing field exists behind a separate interaction or application page;
- whether a page pattern is universal or prevalent.

## Copyright and privacy

Only short observations and labels were recorded. No personal resumes, applicant information or private account content were collected. Contact details visible on a public posting were not used.

## Consequence for interpretation

Findings should be used to improve the research vocabulary and inspection method. They should not yet be turned into product requirements, automated extraction rules, job-quality scores or hiring-status conclusions without a larger, validated sample.
