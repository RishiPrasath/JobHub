# Round 04 remaining-objectives run

Status: complete — 2026-10-19 (Singapore time)

This bounded run completed the Round 04 objectives not covered by the separate job-posting page inventory. It used Singapore-first public-web research, with international evidence retained only when its transfer limits were explicit. No participants were contacted, no private material was extracted, no personal data was uploaded, and no external applications or other submissions were made.

## Evidence posture

High confidence is reserved for official requirements, documented guidance and directly stated tool policies. Medium confidence covers convergent practical guidance and bounded empirical findings. Low confidence covers vendor success claims, anecdotes, prevalence and outcome predictions. This run produces research inputs and hypotheses, not product requirements or architecture decisions.
