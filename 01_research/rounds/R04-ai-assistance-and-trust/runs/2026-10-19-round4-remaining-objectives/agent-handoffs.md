## Coordinator synthesis method

The coordinator kept direct observations, interpretations, hypotheses and product implications separate. Overlaps were reconciled by preferring source-specific claims over universal advice and by preserving contradictions, especially around ATS behaviour, AI outcome effects, worker experience and adoption estimates.
