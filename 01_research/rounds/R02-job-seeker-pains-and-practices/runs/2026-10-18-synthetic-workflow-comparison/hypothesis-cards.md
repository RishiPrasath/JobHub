# Provisional hypothesis cards — Round 02

Round 01 is [not started](../../../01_public-web-user-insights/round-synthesis.md). These are two unranked paper-flow tests using fictional inputs, not claims of demand, observed user behavior, or an accepted product scope. The [Round 00 synthesis](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/round-synthesis.md) explicitly withholds a greatest-burden or first-segment conclusion.

## A. Adjacent-occupation application preparation

- **Problem claim and boundary:** The [friction findings, H1](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-job-search-friction/findings.md) report application uncertainty in sampled graduates and professionals (P01–P03; [source register](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-job-search-friction/sources.md)). Applying that uncertainty to a retail-to-operations switcher is an inference from the [product investigation](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-remaining-research/product-market.md), not direct switcher evidence. Missing feedback may leave the next preparation choice unclear; it has not been shown to cause repeated, avoidable effort. A real SQL requirement or unavailable hours is a separate fit or employment-condition issue that better wording cannot resolve. Confidence: moderate for bounded reported uncertainty, low for this workflow's prevalence or benefit.
- **User outcome:** Decide whether to pursue an adjacent role and prepare one truthful, requirement-linked example, while seeing essential gaps, preferences, unknown terms, the posting version, and a recoverable next action.
- **Manual/no-product alternative:** Keep the posting and a requirement-to-experience table in notes or a spreadsheet; draft a bullet oneself or with a coach, then mark the application state manually.
- **Testable paper-flow hypothesis:** With fictional Jordan and Jobs A/B from the [synthetic fixture](../../../../05_evidence/datasets-and-examples/2026-10-18-synthetic-workflow-check.md), a source-labelled requirement/evidence table plus explicit unknowns will support an honest preparation choice after interruption and after Job A changes to a fixed-term contract.
- **Predicted observations:** The walkthrough distinguishes Job A's spreadsheet requirement from preferred ERP, leaves salary/hours unknown, identifies Job B's mandatory SQL gap, drafts without invented metrics or skills, flags Job A version 2, and returns to a saved-but-not-submitted next action. Compare these checks with the same manual table; no time saving or user preference is implied.
- **Disconfirming observations:** The paper flow implies Jordan has SQL or verified achievements, buries the new contract term, treats missing evidence as ineligibility, loses the saved state, or adds more correction work than the manual table without changing a useful decision.
- **Risks:** A polished application can obscure a material skills or condition mismatch; speculative fit language, stale posting text, and a false “applied” state can mislead. Profile upkeep may exceed any reuse benefit.
- **Next evidence need:** Round 01 public insight and a later consented switcher episode comparison: what actually changed between applications, why, and whether a source-linked preparation aid changes a decision or reduces corrections relative to existing notes/coaching. The [reviewed product result](../../../../04_results/product-and-users/2026-10-18-product-evidence.md) leaves comprehension and maintenance burden open.

## B. Frontline job-condition comparison

- **Problem claim and boundary:** The [friction findings, H3](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-job-search-friction/findings.md) and [reviewed result](../../../../04_results/product-and-users/2026-10-18-job-search-friction.md) support bounded, source-reported difficulty finding feasible work in selected supported-employment/caregiver populations (P05/G02; [source register](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-job-search-friction/sources.md)); employer evidence D01 is context, not worker demand. Extending this to frontline workers generally and treating it as a solvable information problem are untested inferences from the [product investigation](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-remaining-research/product-market.md). An incompatible shift, pay level, commute or physical demand stays incompatible even when clearly displayed; an undisclosed term is missing information, and comparing postings is process effort. Confidence: moderate for bounded feasibility constraints, low for frontline prevalence or product remedy.
- **User outcome:** Compare two roles' stated and unstated hours, contract, pay components, duties and training; distinguish a known conflict from a question for the employer, then choose a defensible next action.
- **Manual/no-product alternative:** Copy each posting into a two-column notes/spreadsheet comparison, mark unknowns, ask an employer or adviser about decisive terms, and record the answer and date.
- **Testable paper-flow hypothesis:** For two fictional frontline postings, a source-labelled condition table will preserve unknown and changed terms, separate a definite incompatibility from information to verify, and recover the comparison and next question after interruption.
- **Predicted observations:** The walkthrough leaves unstated salary/hours blank or “unknown,” identifies any explicitly incompatible shift as a constraint, surfaces a specific question about contract/pay or training, preserves the source version, and returns to a saved-but-not-applied state. Judge against the manual table on clarity and correction effort, not a fabricated decision-speed measure.
- **Disconfirming observations:** The flow calls an unknown shift compatible, recommends preparation for a known unworkable schedule, converts an employer claim or isolated complaint into verified fact, loses a changed term, or adds complexity without helping the person distinguish “decline” from “ask first.”
- **Risks:** False precision about total pay or hours, stale postings, and implied eligibility can cause poor choices. A comparison aid cannot alter wages, scheduling, access barriers or employer conduct; assisted/offline, language and device needs remain under-researched.
- **Next evidence need:** Round 01 insight and later consented frontline/returner episodes establishing current intent and availability, when decisive conditions became known, whether a choice changed because information was missing or misunderstood, and how a manual comparison performs. The [friction limitations](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-job-search-friction/limitations.md) caution that worker voices and actual effort measures remain thin.
