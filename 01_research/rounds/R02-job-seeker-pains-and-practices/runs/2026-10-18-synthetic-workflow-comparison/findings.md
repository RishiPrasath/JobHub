## Disagreement, counterexamples, and what could change the reading

- The [scenario B card](scenario-cards.md) and [B6 flow](flows.md) describe the new late shifts as a *potential* conflict pending weekday placement. The [coordinator trace](observations.md) and [UX Expert review](ux-review.md) independently resolve the fictional constraint as a **definite conflict under the stated availability**. Employer flexibility remains unknown; asking about an exception is valid, but asking only which days are assigned cannot resolve the given two-close requirement.
- The initial [A4 trace](observations.md) correctly notes no SQL, while [A4 flow](flows.md) uses broader “confirmed absence” wording. The [UX review](ux-review.md) exposed the ambiguity and the [post-review rubric application](observations.md) records it as Concern. “No evidence supplied for three years of analytics” must not become “Jordan confirmed none.”
- Both candidates have a simpler counterexample: the [manual baseline walkthrough](observations.md) records essential facts with rows and notes. There was no actual comparison of comprehension, effort or preference, so neither proposed flow has demonstrated added value. B's error also shows why attractive source labels alone are insufficient; the interpretation and its correction path matter. [D9–D12 ratings](observations.md), [UX review 5](ux-review.md).
- A later independent, consented test could reverse a provisional preference if people cannot distinguish unknown from absent, miss the schedule conflict, cannot recover a partial save, or find the structured flow more work than notes. Evidence from [Round 01](../../../01_public-web-user-insights/round-synthesis.md) could change which problem deserves the next investment. These are disconfirming conditions, not observed participant results. [Hypothesis cards](hypothesis-cards.md), [rubric challenge cases](rubric.md).

## Bounded next test and handoff

**One primary next test:** after correcting the B6/B7 paper copy and the A4/partial-capture labels in a separately authorised design pass, run one small **low-fidelity comprehension and recovery comparison** using the same fictional A and B cards and a manual two-row baseline. Ask people to explain the known conflict versus an unknown term, identify which of Jordan's claims is confirmed versus merely unsupported, pause/return after a partial save, correct one fact, and state whether anything was applied. Record unaided explanations, incorrect decisions, correction steps and added work by case, without a single winner score. If access to consented participants remains unavailable, an independent paper review can check corrected logic but cannot replace participant evidence. This is a test proposal, not approval of a product scope or a platform.
