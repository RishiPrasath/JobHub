## Claim map and counterevidence

- **Preparation uncertainty (A):** S02 reports uncertainty in bounded graduate/professional samples. S03 proposes adjacent-role evidence work. The causal step from uncertainty to repeated avoidable preparation is unproven; a simple manual note or human coach may already help.
- **Conditions and missing information (B):** S02 supports bounded constraints on feasible work in selected groups. S03 proposes comparing shifts, pay components, duties and training. Clearer information cannot make an incompatible condition feasible; the exercise must separate actual conflict from missing or misunderstood facts.
- **Interrupted capture and return (both):** S04 supplies a design hypothesis and documented platform considerations. There is no observed frequency or measured recovery benefit for JobHub users; the comparison uses interruption as a stress case, not a ranked user problem.
- **Truthfulness and evidence (both):** S04 and S05 define risks around unstated salary, inferred qualifications, source changes, and false submission state. The paper exercise can reveal logical flaws in proposed flows, but cannot measure model accuracy or real-world comprehension.
