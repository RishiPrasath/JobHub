# Agent-run paper walkthrough observations

Walked on 2026-10-18 by the JobHub coordinator (Codex), after the [common rubric](rubric.md) was written and before findings were synthesized. The product owner did not perform this walkthrough. Inputs are fictional [scenario cards](scenario-cards.md); the artifact inspected is the proposed [paper flows](flows.md). “Observed” below means text and state transitions visible in those documents. No software, device, browser, model, assistive technology, job seeker, employer, or live posting was exercised.

## B — frontline condition comparison

Initial fictional state: Mira has found SYNTH-FRONT-A and B v1; neither is applied to. Mira finishes weekday work by 18:00 and can work one weekend day. The task is to save two postings, leave, return, compare conditions, inspect A v2 and expiry, and choose a next action.

|---|---|---|---|

| B2 | Interrupt with both records saved and comparison unfinished. | [B2](flows.md) keeps two independent save acknowledgements; partial save does not claim both succeeded. | Actual persistence is untested. Next action: resume comparison. |

| B4 | Separate known shifts, pay/contract, required credentials, preferred first aid, and unknowns. | [B4](flows.md) keeps A v1 schedule unresolved, shows B's 08:00–17:00, pay range and six-month term, and marks B's forklift/stock gaps. | The state is clearer than a single fit score, but the paper does not show the effort of entering/reviewing every field. Next action: ask or stop. |
| B5 | Draft questions only. | [B5](flows.md) prepares questions about A's days/pay/contract and B's alternative qualifications; no message is sent. | A direct manual note can do the same. Next action: copy/defer/stop. |

Challenge branches inspected: A v1's finish times, pay and contract stay unknown; B's first-aid status is candidate-unstated while the certificate is only preferred. B has a known schedule that fits Mira's stated weekday boundary but a known material qualification/experience gap. A's malicious line is specified as inert, not technically tested. Confirmed expiry and failed check have separate paper states. B6 provides the required failure case.

## Manual notes/spreadsheet baseline

I followed the [scenario cards' baseline](scenario-cards.md) with the same fictional facts. A row per posting could contain source/version, stated requirements or conditions, unknowns, confirmed candidate facts, next question, last check, and saved/applied state. A separate note could hold Jordan's truthful bullet or Mira's employer questions. After interruption, the person reopens the rows; after A changes, they compare old and new copies manually. This paper baseline preserves the same essential decision information if kept carefully. It also carries manual work to retain the source text, notice changes, and maintain state. No actual spreadsheet, timing, comprehension test, or user preference was observed, so the comparison cannot establish that either proposed JobHub flow is better.

## Observed design issues and limits

- B6 is the clearest failure: the proposed interpretation conflicts with its own fictional availability and shift facts. It requires correction before using this flow as a prototype script.
- A and B both specify honest source/status language and recovery paths, but persistence, refresh, export, focus order, reflow, screen-reader output, and security boundaries are not enacted by paper text.
- Both workflows require source capture, version checks, candidate confirmation, and correction. The owner effort is visible as a sequence of review decisions, not measured time.
- The public user-insight study remains unexecuted, so this walkthrough cannot say which problem matters more to job seekers.

## Post-review correction to this record

After the initial walkthrough, the independent [UX Expert review](ux-review.md) identified two additional provenance ambiguities and incomplete recovery detail. The D1, D2, D3, and D7 categories above were revised from their initial paper reading to **Concern** where applicable; the predeclared rubric did not change. Jordan explicitly lacks SQL experience, but the supplied history does not explicitly establish the absence of three years of analytics work. A link-only or partial capture cannot justify “not stated in the posting.” These are document-level findings to correct before a prototype script is reused, not observations of user behavior.
