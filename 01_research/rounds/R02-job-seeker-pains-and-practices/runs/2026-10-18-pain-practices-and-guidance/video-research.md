Read `AGENTS.md`, `_agents/README.md`, `_agents/04_job-seeker-practices-researcher.md`, `_agents/17_research-query-strategist.md`, this run's `brief.md`, and the round's `query-plan-draft.md`. Six additional candidate URL checks count against the eight-candidate cap, including one inaccessible, incorrectly capitalised URL. Five canonical additional videos were captured. No audio listening or audiovisual verification was performed. Every high-impact claim below remains **unverified against audio**, including numbers, negations, role titles, legal/platform statements, and outcome accounts. Manual subtitle availability does not establish transcription accuracy.

Evidence labels:

- **Advice/interpretation:** a creator's suggested practice or explanation; not evidence that job seekers generally behave this way or that it works.
- **Personal account:** the speaker describes their own experience; externally uncorroborated. **Secondhand account:** a speaker describes someone else's experience.
- **Demonstration described in captions:** narration indicates a worked example; screen contents and execution have not been visually checked.
- **Statistic pending primary-source verification:** a number attributed to research or asserted by the speaker. No population, causal, market-rate, or success-rate claim is accepted from these videos alone.

All entries below paraphrase the captions. Timestamp ranges are navigation windows, not verbatim quotations. Advice can generate questions and candidate practices; it cannot settle product scope, employer wrongdoing, legal obligations, or market value. International material is transfer evidence, not Singapore validation.
