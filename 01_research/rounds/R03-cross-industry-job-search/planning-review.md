## Method-only checks

The coordinator checked official [SSIC](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/standard-classifications/national-classifications/singapore-standard-industrial-classification-ssic) and [SSOC](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/standard-classifications/national-classifications/singapore-standard-occupational-classification-ssoc) landing pages during planning. They support keeping economic activity and occupation distinct; the 13 baskets are our purposive grouping, not formal codes. Earlier guessed routes returned 404 and were not treated as inspected reports. See [sector matrix](sector-matrix.md).

The credibility specialist also inspected these methodological references during planning; the later research run must verify currency and record its own inspected passages before using them as findings:

- [BLS JOLTS concepts](https://www.bls.gov/opub/hom/jlt/concepts.htm): a US statistical job-opening definition, not a Singapore legal test or a ghost-posting detector. Vacancy stock and hiring flow cannot simply be subtracted to count ghost jobs.
- [Google JobPosting guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting): useful distinctions concerning expired or nonexistent jobs and impersonation. A platform policy is not evidence of compliance or prevalence.
- [Indeed job-feed guidance](https://docs.indeed.com/job-sync-xml/xml-feed): a source for a platform's stated feed requirements, not proof of actual employer intent; syndication and multiple locations require case-level interpretation.
- [Clarify Capital ghost-jobs survey](https://clarifycapital.com/ghost-jobs): a candidate commercial self-report study for methods scrutiny, not an accepted prevalence estimate. Inspect definitions, question wording, recruitment, population, geography, time frame and missing methods before retaining any claim.

Checks occurred during planning on 18–19 October 2026. They are not the 66-query execution log. No evidence-collection run, monitoring schedule, employer contact or test application has begun.
