## 1. Research question and decision context

How do Singapore-first job-search workflows, information needs, constraints, channels, and workarounds vary across industries, occupations, and working arrangements, particularly outside IT? What public evidence distinguishes misleading or inactive postings from scams, ordinary recruitment processes, and corroborated cases of no current hiring intent?

The decision informed is what contrasts and uncertainties deserve subsequent research or a reversible workflow experiment. This convenience corpus cannot rank sector demand, pain, opportunity, hiring probability, or product priority. Product scope, platform, architecture, privacy decisions, and final acceptance remain with the human owner.

Searchable questions:

- Q1: How do people discover, assess, save, prepare, apply, interview or demonstrate skills, track, follow up, and return to opportunities?
- Q2: Which conditions matter across occupation and arrangement: shifts, roster, pay components, commute or worksite, credentials, portfolio, practical trials, language, device access, agency involvement, or fragmented information?
- Q3: What works well, including referrals, walk-ins, public services, specialist channels, manual tracking, and existing tools? When is the proposed difficulty absent or manageable?
- Q4: What evidence supports a posting's reported status or intent, what alternative explanations exist, and what can a seeker actually verify from public information?
- Q5: Where are retrieval gaps, source dependence, and geographic or temporal transfer limits too large for comparison?

### Posting-status and intent definitions

Maintain separate fields for authenticity, posting status, reported intent, supporting evidence, and unresolved alternatives. A case may have more than one status; never collapse everything into a single “ghost” label.

| Category | Working definition / evidence sought | What it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| Scam / impersonation | Deceptive recruitment or a falsely represented employer/recruiter; seek official alerts or independently supported identity evidence. | An authentic employer's lack of immediate hiring intent. |
| Expired / stale | A closed, outdated, or unsynchronised listing remains visible. | Deliberate deception or original intent. |
| Reposted | The same or similar advert appears again; determine whether this is syndication, refresh, multiple vacancies, or a new requisition if possible. | An unfilled role, fake vacancy, or intent, based on repetition alone. |
| Evergreen / talent pool | Ongoing pipeline or future-interest collection, whether clearly disclosed or ambiguous. | Immediate vacancy, deceptive intent, or equivalence to every other pipeline. |
| Freeze / cancellation | Recruitment paused or the vacancy withdrawn; distinguish intention at publication from a later change. | Lack of original hiring intent. |
| Internal fill | A role filled internally or an internal candidate considered; seek timing and documented explanation. | A sham external process merely because an internal candidate exists. |
| Corroborated no-current-intent | Evidence tied to a defined employer/posting or defined study sample and time shows no plan to hire currently, with corroboration beyond an unsupported allegation. | Population prevalence, wrongdoing, or intent of another employer or posting. |
| Unknown / disputed | Intent or status cannot be established, sources disagree, or only suspicion is available. | Permission to select the most alarming explanation. |

Never infer hiring intent from employer silence, rejection, posting age, repeated adverts, applicant counts, or a long hiring process. An explicit pipeline disclosure can corroborate non-immediate intent without implying misconduct. For a stronger intent claim, seek a dated, attributable employer statement or a transparent primary investigation/study linked to the relevant posting or defined sample, plus independent supporting context. If this standard is unavailable, retain “reported” or “unverified” rather than upgrading the claim. Republished coverage is not independent corroboration.

Keep employer-reported no-immediate-intent separate from independently corroborated misleading-vacancy claims; an admission or disclosed pipeline does not by itself establish deception. No numerical fake-job probability, company blacklist or phrase-based accusation is an output. Consequential intent claims require separate independent review under the parent assignments.

## 5. Source strategy and inclusion rules

Prefer public primary research with disclosed method, official Singapore statistics/service/regulator documentation, and attributable employer or platform statements for their own processes. Candidate official discovery routes include MOM, SingStat, WSG, e2i and relevant sector bodies. Their suitability and currency must be verified during execution; their appearance here is not a claim that a particular source exists.

Pay, eligibility, labour rules, licence requirements and platform policies need authoritative current sources with the relevant role and jurisdiction identified. This is evidence gathering, with no personalised legal, salary or employment-rights advice. Public availability does not authorise contact, deanonymisation or collection of private details; respect source-access restrictions and rate limits. Reused prior sources retain their original qualified IDs, dates and family identity.

Seek detailed public firsthand accounts for what a person reports doing or experiencing. Public forums, blogs, reviews, community discussions and support threads may illuminate sequence and workarounds but are self-selected and often unverifiable. Preserve positive cases and contrary explanations. No source-family quota guarantees that a basket will have all evidence types.

Academic/qualitative material should expose population, recruitment, geography, fieldwork period and method. Distinguish observed practice, self-report, practitioner guidance, employer process claims, platform documentation and vendor marketing. Product reviews and competitor documentation are eligible only for a mapped channel/workflow question, not an unbounded feature/pricing inventory. Review volume cannot establish product quality or unmet demand.

Include sources with inspectable public content, relevant context and a traceable date or explicit `date unknown` label. Exclude or retain solely as access/discovery notes: snippets, generated summaries, inaccessible full texts, SEO aggregations without provenance, duplicates and unsupported allegations. Follow citations to the primary work where freely available; do not bypass restrictions.

Video is optional only when free public captions/transcripts are available and the material answers a remaining question better than accessible text. Log caption availability and whether captions are automatic, record timestamps for relevant paraphrases, and check consequential wording against the accessible video where feasible. No paid transcription, mandatory video count or private video access. A video search costs a query; a caption access failure is a method limitation, not missing evidence of a phenomenon.
