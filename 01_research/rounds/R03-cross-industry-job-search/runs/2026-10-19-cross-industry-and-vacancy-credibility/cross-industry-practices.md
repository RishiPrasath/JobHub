# Cross-industry practices and alternative routes

Accessed 2026-10-19. These are bounded source-reported practices, not observed behaviour, prevalence or proven outcomes.

## Reported AI practice versus a prototype

R3-P02's opening author says they are experimenting, are not actively seeking work, and have not finished the use case. Separate commenters claim personal use of AI for matching, tailoring, applications and tracking. One describes keeping a master resume and revising generated outputs. Do not merge those people into one successful journey or treat claimed callback counts as causal evidence.

The thread's discussion of scraping, contact lookup, salary targets and auto-applications does not establish permission, current provider capabilities, factual accuracy, compliance or good practice. This round did not reproduce those workflows or upload any personal data. A commenter's cutoff for calling an application “ghosted” is not proof that a job was fake.

Original: [R3-P02 public discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/asksg/comments/1w9rfbb/have_anyone_every_seen_or_heard_anyone_apply_for/). Occupations and individual locations are unclear, so these practices cannot fill the healthcare, warehouse or other sector-specific AI-use gaps.

## Manual and human alternatives

Breadth records R3-M02 and R3-M04 include outlet QR/walk-in application reports. R3-M14 is an employer-curated career-switch account. R3-P01 attributes senior design opportunities to introductions and existing trust, but the source promotes a service and supplies no method for its comparative numerical claims. Retain the narrow reported route, not the implied universal superiority of referrals.

Original: [R3-P01 design practitioner post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/josephlouistan_how-i-landed-senior-roles-without-applying-activity-7364280286420881408-ty5t). Singapore-linked author/institution does not establish the location or accessibility of every role.

## Retrieval gap

Three added queries tested nurse AI application use, warehouse AI application use and design referral success. They did not establish occupation-specific Singapore nurse or warehouse AI-use sequences. Clinical AI adoption studies answer a different question and were excluded. This is a retrieval limit, not evidence of non-use.

Interpretation: any eventual JobHub concept should compare with manual and human alternatives and preserve user review. Whether it improves effort, accuracy or outcomes remains untested and belongs in later analysis.
