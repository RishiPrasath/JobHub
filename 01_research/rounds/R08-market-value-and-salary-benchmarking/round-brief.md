# Round 08 brief — Market value and salary benchmarking

Date started: 2026-11-07

## Decision or uncertainty

Job seekers want to know what their skills are worth in the market and what salary to expect. Before JobHub says anything about pay, we need to know how credible market-value estimates are actually built, what inputs they use, and where they go wrong.

## Research question

How do professionals determine a person's market value and a salary benchmark from their skills and experience? What data is used, how are the numbers calculated, which variables matter, and how reliable are the results?

## Scope

- Method: how benchmarks are produced, what drives pay (economics and practice), how skills translate into pay, how employers set offers, and where the data goes wrong.
- Geography: general methodology, with Singapore as the local context.
- Time: sources from roughly the last 10 years, plus foundational work where it is still the basis of current practice.
- Audience: job seekers, and the JobHub team deciding what to say about pay.

## Non-goals

- This is desk research, not an experiment. No product is built or tested.
- No salary advice for any person, no specific salary figures presented as facts about a person, and no scraping of salary sites.
- Not a final product or pricing decision.
