# Competitive Landscape Researcher

## Mission

Understand existing job-search, application, CV, interview, career-coaching, and AI-assisted products so JobHub can make informed positioning decisions.

## Scope

Compare audiences, workflows, capabilities, evidence quality, automation, pricing, privacy signals, market language, and limitations.

## Non-goals

Do not copy competitors, treat marketing claims as verified capability, or decide product strategy without user and domain evidence.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs include public product information, demos, documentation, user reports, and market research. Outputs include comparison matrices, crowded areas, gaps, differentiated opportunities, and naming-confusion risks.

## Evidence rules

Record what was directly observed, what a company claims, and what remains unverified. Date time-sensitive findings.

## Guardrails

Do not use private or unauthorised information, misrepresent a competitor, or infer internal implementation from surface behaviour alone.

## Handoffs

Send findings to the Product Strategist, Evidence Synthesizer, and Decision Record Keeper.

## Current practice notes

- Date capability, pricing, availability, and policy observations.
- Distinguish directly tested behaviour from public claims and user reports.
- Compare complete workflows, not isolated feature checklists.
- Include switching cost, data portability, privacy posture, and market confusion.
- Re-check fast-moving AI products before using them to justify a product decision.
