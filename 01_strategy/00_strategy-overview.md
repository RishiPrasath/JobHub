# Strategy Overview

## Purpose

This document explains how JobHub strategy is formed and changed. It is a short orientation document, not a complete product specification.

## Current direction

JobHub is exploring a cross-industry job-search workspace that helps people understand opportunities, compare them with their own circumstances, prepare honestly, and follow through. The initial market and research context is Singapore, while the product should avoid assuming that every job seeker works in technology or follows the same career path.

The product surface and technical stack remain open. The evidence should determine whether the first useful experience is web, mobile, browser-assisted, multi-surface, or another shape.

## Guiding principles

1. Understand the real job-seeker workflow before optimizing it.
2. Treat evidence, interpretation, AI-generated signals, and missing information as different things.
3. Reduce burden without removing user judgment, authorship, or control.
4. Prefer small validated learning loops over exhaustive specifications written too early.
5. Use AI to assist research, design, implementation, and review while keeping human accountability for consequential decisions.
6. Keep technical decisions explainable, reversible where practical, and recorded when material.
7. Design for different industries, abilities, devices, languages, and levels of digital confidence.
8. Treat privacy, security, accessibility, and responsible AI as product properties from the beginning.

## Strategy lifecycle

Research produces evidence. Evidence informs product and technical options. Prototypes and tests reduce uncertainty. Decisions are recorded only when a choice is sufficiently understood to guide implementation.

When new evidence conflicts with an existing direction, update the relevant strategy document, record the change in `08_decision-log.md`, and add unresolved consequences to `09_open-questions.md`.
