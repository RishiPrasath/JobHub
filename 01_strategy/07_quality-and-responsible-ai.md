# Quality and Responsible AI

## Quality goals

JobHub should be useful, understandable, evidence-grounded, accessible, privacy-conscious, secure, and maintainable.

## Evaluation areas

- Factual accuracy and source preservation.
- Clear separation of facts, interpretations, signals, and missing information.
- Appropriate uncertainty and confidence communication.
- Helpfulness and relevance for different industries and user groups.
- Truthfulness and user control in application content.
- Accessibility and usability across devices.
- Privacy, security, and retention behavior.
- Cost, latency, reliability, and quality drift after model or prompt changes.

## Human approval boundaries

Human review should be required before consequential external actions, including sending applications or messages, making public claims about companies or people, changing durable user data, or executing high-impact integrations.

## Failure handling

The system should make it easy to inspect sources, correct information, disagree with a recommendation, undo changes where possible, and report unsafe or misleading output.
