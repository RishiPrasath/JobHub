## Headline
A salary benchmark is built from pay data matched to jobs by duties, cleaned, brought to a common date and reported as percentiles; a person's value is that benchmark adjusted for their experience, scarce skills and scope, and then limited by what a given employer's band and budget allow. Skills add modest, uneven premiums (about 3% on average for a new skill in advertised pay; more for a few groups); employer, education and experience matter as much or more. Salary data is biased in known ways, so the sound approach is to triangulate several source types and show a range with its basis.
