# Round 01 limitations — bounded public-web first pass

Date: 2026-10-18. Read with this run's [findings](findings.md), [sources](sources.md), [observations](observations.md), and [query log](query-log.md). These limits apply to every H1/H2/H3 comparison.

## What the evidence cannot establish

- No researcher in this run observed a job seeker completing a real search or application. Public posts are unverified accounts; studies and surveys are source-reported results. This was not direct user research, usability testing, or validation of a JobHub concept.
- There is no comparable measure of active effort, waiting, confidence, abandonment, or lost opportunity across hypotheses, stages, occupations, or Singapore populations. Counts of retrieved pages, threads, respondents, or application attempts cannot rank problem severity or prevalence.
- The record does not establish causality, product demand, intervention effectiveness, hiring impact, or a preferred feature, platform, automation level, segment, or product scope.
- H3's specific Singapore phone-discovery → laptop-return → lost-opportunity chain was not found. A desktop accessibility blocker, a preference for desktop applications, or difficult follow-up is **not** evidence of that chain. Failure to retrieve it does not show the experience never happens.

## Source dependence and transfer

- S03 repeats Round 00 D01's MOM employer survey family. Its large employer response base supports only the survey's employer-side statements; it cannot corroborate applicant experience independently or show that a posting omitted conditions.
- S04 is a vendor-published Singapore survey of employees **and** job seekers. The opened page does not disclose recruitment or a job-seeker-only denominator. Its flexibility figure is a stated intention, not an observed decline or a non-tech subgroup rate.
- S12 is a vendor-published UK/US panel, not Singapore behavioral evidence. Self-reported AI use/non-use cannot demonstrate effects of AI assistance. S04 and S12 are different survey families but do not answer the same precise question.
- S05, S06, S08, and S09 are Singapore forum accounts; S02, S10, and S11 are other forum accounts. Different threads on one platform are not automatically independent samples. Authors, circumstances, outcomes, and promotion incentives are incompletely verified. Replies in one thread remain one thread family.
- S07's 20 lower-qualified unemployed interviewees were in Spain; fieldwork began in 2020 although publication was in 2024. Its reported platform practices transfer only as a possible mechanism to investigate in Singapore, not as a current Singapore rate or cross-sector estimate.
- S01 involved three expert blind screen-reader users, 30 selected U.S. company sites, and desktop test combinations. The sampled task outcome is meaningful within that design but does not measure phone use, cross-device continuation, Singapore portals, or all access needs.

## Coverage and retrieval limits

- Searchable, English-language public material favors people who post about difficulties and excludes many assisted, offline, informal, and non-English routes. Graduate, office, and tech narratives remain easier to retrieve than contemporary first-person service, trades, logistics, cleaning, and healthcare-support sequences.
- The opened Singapore material is thin on non-tech pre-application decisions: when salary, roster, location, duties, or eligibility became known; whether the ad already stated them; and what the applicant did after learning them. S05/S06 have no captured original ad or confirmed final decision.
- Career changers, returners, mature or retrenched workers, people with low digital confidence, and people with access needs are not adequately represented as Singapore firsthand episodes. No characteristic should be inferred from a forum post.
- Q8's broad government search and narrow ResearchGate route retained no direct switching study. Q5/Q6 searches returned adjacent guidance, a 404, redirect/access failures, and listings with no usable text. One thread timed out and another page returned an internal error. Search snippets and inaccessible pages were not evidence. These are retrieval limits, not disproof.
- Search date and source publication date differ; some forum dates rely on search indexing or relative-page display. Public pages may change. The run preserved links and paraphrases, not immutable copies of every page.
- The query log records attempts and gaps but not complete elapsed time or a documented judgment that another 30-minute block would be uninformative. The planned stopping rule therefore cannot be certified from the current file set.

## Claims withheld and unresolved distinctions

- H1: Re-entering data or revising materials does not by itself show avoidable work. Missing feedback, uncertain eligibility, site design, and chosen tailoring may each explain a different episode. AI correction in a forum reply is not a measured failure rate.
- H2: A practical condition can be incompatible even if fully disclosed. An employer's perception, a worker's stated preference, late discovery of a location, and an actual decision to decline are different claims. The current ledger cannot determine which condition was missing, misunderstood, negotiable, or decisive.
- H3: Saved-job tracking, application follow-up, phone application, desktop preference, accessibility, and cross-device handoff must stay separate. A simple email or spreadsheet routine may suffice for some; applying immediately by phone is another counterexample. Neither proves there is no interruption problem for others.
- Round 00's Singapore feasibility evidence and S03's repeated employer report should not be summed with this pass as fresh independent corroboration. Round 00's hypothesis numbers also refer to different questions.
