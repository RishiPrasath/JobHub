## Answer within this pass

The material makes all three candidate problems worth keeping as **questions**, but it does not establish which causes the greatest loss of time, confidence, or opportunity for Singapore job seekers. The most precise version of H3 — finding an opportunity on a phone, intending to continue on a laptop, then losing that opportunity because the handoff fails — is **unproved**. No retrieved account documents that full Singapore sequence. Source counts are not a priority or prevalence measure.

Here, **source report** means what an opened study, survey, employer, or public poster says; **researcher inference** means this run's interpretation of those reports; **prior evidence** means Round 00 material, not a new Round 01 source; and **unknown** means the record cannot establish the claim. Confidence concerns the bounded statement in its row, not transfer to all users.

### Cross-cutting AI and access observations

Source reports describe both using and checking AI output (S08–S10/O08–O10); S12/O12's UK/US vendor panel reports both AI and non-AI application behavior. This is evidence of varied reported practice, not AI effectiveness or universal adoption. S01's expert task evaluation supports an accessibility barrier in its selected U.S. desktop sites; it cannot be used as evidence that a JobHub interface, a mobile workflow, or a Singapore application flow is usable or unusable.

## Independence and relationship to Round 00

- S07 is one disclosed-method Spanish interview family; S01 is a separate U.S. expert task-evaluation family, but its outcome does not test H3's device handoff. S04 and S12 are separate vendor-reported surveys with different populations and claims. Multiple Reddit posts and replies supply useful wording and counterexamples, but the self-selected platform material does not establish independent, representative corroboration merely by appearing in several threads.
- S03 is the **same MOM Job Vacancies 2025 family as Round 00 D01**. It adds no independent source family to the [Round 00 job-search-friction synthesis](../../../00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-job-search-friction/findings.md). It is employer-side context, not applicant testimony or an audit of posting fields.
- Numbering changed between runs. Round 00 **H1** (feedback and repeated preparation) overlaps part of this run's H1. Round 00 **H3** (feasible working conditions) overlaps this run's H2, but its P05/G02 bounded feasibility evidence does not prove missing or late information. Round 00 **H2** (assessment effort before an application ends) is not this run's H2. Round 00 already marked device access and cross-device continuity as gaps; this pass has not closed the precise H3 gap.
- The Round 00 findings and limitations are prior synthesis, not extra Round 01 observations. Their conclusion that uncertainty and feasible conditions exist in bounded populations does not rank this run's three candidates or validate a solution.

## Stop disposition and next handoff

This bounded first pass has an explicit gap or counterexample for each candidate, but it does **not** demonstrate every planned stopping condition: the query log does not certify elapsed blocks or that another block could not change the shortlist, and no precise H3 handoff claim meets the independent-family condition for a synthetic test. Major non-tech, access, and offline stages remain thin. These are run-level findings for the coordinator's separate round-level synthesis and gate; query volume does not settle that gate.

**One focused next research question:** In a recent Singapore job-search episode where a person found a role on a phone and planned to continue on a laptop, what happened between discovery, saving, return, and the decision to apply or stop — and was any opportunity actually lost because of that transition? This addresses an explicit evidence gap; it does not rank H3 or prescribe a prototype.
