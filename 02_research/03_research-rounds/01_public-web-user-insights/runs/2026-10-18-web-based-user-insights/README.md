# Web-based user insights

This activity is designed for a solo founder without direct access to users. It gathers publicly available reports of job-search experiences from the internet; it does not claim to conduct interviews, usability testing, or representative user research.

## Status

Bounded public-web first pass conducted and synthesized 2026-10-18; owner acceptance of any product conclusion remains open. The [query plan](query-plan.md) was prepared before evidence collection and reviewed by the Research Query Strategist. The Job Domain Researcher, Job-Seeker Practices Researcher, and coordinator's UX/access pass opened sources and recorded them in the [query log](query-log.md), [source ledger](sources.md), and [observation ledger](observations.md). The Evidence Synthesizer produced [findings](findings.md) and [limitations](limitations.md). This is public-source review, not interviews or usability testing. The specific Singapore phone-to-laptop lost-opportunity sequence remains unestablished.

## Purpose

Identify recurring job-search problems, AI-use patterns, workarounds, frustrations, and unmet needs from publicly available user-generated material and published research. Use the findings to choose which product hypothesis deserves a synthetic workflow or low-fidelity prototype.

## Planned outputs

The executed run will use this exact file set:

- `README.md` — status, purpose, role order, and relationship to later work
- `brief.md` — decision, scope, exclusions, pass budget, and stopping rule
- `query-plan.md` — Research Query Strategist vocabulary map, query portfolio, source strategy, retrieval risks, and stopping rule
- `query-log.md` — attempted query families, venues, dates, result quality, useful source IDs, redundancy, failures, and follow-up terms
- `sources.md` — source ledger with URL, date, platform, context, method, source family, evidence type, and access date
- `observations.md` — observation ledger separating direct source statements from researcher interpretation
- `findings.md` — theme matrix, contradictions, negative cases, confidence, and hypotheses for synthetic workflow testing
- `limitations.md` — evidence-quality, sampling, transfer, access, and explicitly withheld claims

## Assigned research roles

- Research Planner: owns the question, scope, evidence standard, and stopping decision.
- Research Query Strategist: works first after the Planner and prepares the query portfolio, vocabulary map, source-family coverage, query log, and retrieval-risk notes before evidence collection.
- Job-Seeker Practices Researcher: investigates public accounts of workflows, workarounds, abandonment, and AI use.
- Job Domain Researcher: investigates job-search stages, occupations, labour-market context, and job-posting language.
- UX Expert or UX and User-Flow Designer: investigates publicly documented device, accessibility, cognitive-load, and interaction-friction patterns.
- Evidence Synthesizer: reconciles source families, contradictions, confidence, and limitations after collection.

The output must not identify private individuals, reproduce large portions of copyrighted material, or treat public posts as permission to contact, profile, or market to their authors. The private Job Hunt workspace is excluded from this activity; earlier internal evidence must remain separate from the public source ledger.

## Relationship to later work

This activity can identify hypotheses and language users use to describe their problems. It cannot prove demand, usability, willingness to pay, or the best product surface. Use the output to select a synthetic workflow, not to declare an MVP validated.
