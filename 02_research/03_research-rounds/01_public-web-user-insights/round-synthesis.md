## What changed from Round 00

- **Preparation/checking (H1)** remains plausible: a disclosed-method Spanish qualitative study and bounded public accounts describe re-entry, unclear profile fields, résumé approaches and checking. Singapore evidence is mostly selected public accounts; a general résumé and selective tailoring are counterexamples to universal per-role preparation.
- **Practical conditions (H2)** remain consequential in bounded contexts: a Singapore employer survey reports perceived schedules/pay/physical-work issues, a vendor survey reports flexibility preferences, and two public accounts show both late location disclosure and a shift pattern known at offer stage. These do not show how commonly advertisements omit terms or whether information, incompatibility, or negotiation determines the outcome. The MOM survey repeats a Round 00 source family.
- **Interruption/return (H3)** has public accounts of context tracking and task-dependent device preferences, but no well-contextualized Singapore account of the complete phone discovery → laptop return → lost opportunity chain. An immediate phone application and workable email/PDF/spreadsheet routines are counterexamples to a universal deferred-handoff need.
- **AI and access** are cross-cutting: some public accounts describe AI-assisted cover letters, interview preparation, checking and correction, while other accounts or a panel report include non-AI approaches. An expert U.S. desktop screen-reader study documents barriers in sampled application sites, not JobHub usability or cross-device behavior.

The run does **not** rank these questions by prevalence, burden, demand, or likely product benefit. Multiple threads on one forum are not independent representative samples. Source statements, prior Round 00 findings, and our interpretations remain distinct in the run files. The initial brief's full stopping rule cannot be certified: time blocks were not logged, Singapore non-tech first-person sequences remain thin, and the precise H3 handoff lacks independent support. This is a transparent stop after a bounded pass, not a claim of saturation.
