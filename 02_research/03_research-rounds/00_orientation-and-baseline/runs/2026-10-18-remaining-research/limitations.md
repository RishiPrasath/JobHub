# Limitations and completion boundary

Date: 2026-10-18.

## Completed work versus unperformed validation

The run covers the remaining desk-research topics, incorporating two earlier studies. It does not contain new participant interviews, observation, usability sessions, device demos, paid-product trials, model comparisons, application submissions, implementation tests or production audits. Proposed fixtures, scores and gates are plans, not results. Source registers state exactly which document sections were inspected.

The owner has not accepted a platform, stack, segment, business model, retention policy, native-agent setup or development-method experiment. Suggestions have not been silently inserted as accepted strategy. Existing source files and unrelated work remain intact.

## Evidence weaknesses

- The prior workflow corpus includes dated, population-specific studies, vendor surveys and anonymous first-person accounts; it does not measure representative current Singapore AI use.
- The prior friction study separates conditions, uncertainty and process effort and explicitly cannot rank burden. A software product cannot itself change unworkable conditions or hidden employer decisions.
- Internal Job Hunt documents show intended process, not adherence, time savings or generalisable user demand. The existing bounded scan was reused rather than expanding into private profiles.
- Product capability and pricing pages establish documented offerings only. No comparative accuracy, export, privacy-implementation or outcome claims were tested.
- Naming overlap is a public screening signal, not trademark clearance or proof of consumer confusion. No domains, marks, store names or handles were reserved.
- Official technical documents can change. Current documentation may differ from an installed version or account entitlement; local Codex configuration was not installed or smoke-tested.
- Browser capability evidence includes historical and archived pages and a current issue status. Actual OS/browser combinations and user entry payloads need testing before a surface decision.
- Selected accessibility criteria are not a full conformance assessment. Neither automated checks nor synthetic personas substitute for disabled users' participation and specialist evaluation.
- AI productivity results differ by tasks, participants, tools and study design. Vendor success stories and perceived speed are not local causal evidence.

## Access limits

All three user-supplied AI-SDLC article texts were accessible to the development researcher; embedded illustrations were not independently analysed. DORA's full report was form-gated; accessible primary analysis and questionnaire were used. The technical researcher read PDPC landing-page summaries but not the full guideline PDFs. OWASP's 2026 download failed, so the report explicitly uses inspected 2025 material. Some SkillsFuture/career-centre routes and Apple's current web-push page were blocked or JavaScript-only; qualified alternative sources are recorded. No unseen contents were inferred from search snippets.

## Regulatory and provider boundaries

The legal/privacy discussion is conditional and source-scoped, not professional advice or a compliance determination. Capture access, copying, storage, transfer and republication can have different constraints. Provider policies differ by consumer/API product, tier, region, feature, model and agreement. No account eligibility or contractual exception was verified. Decisions involving minors, regulated occupational advice, international transfers or public employer allegations need explicit additional review.
