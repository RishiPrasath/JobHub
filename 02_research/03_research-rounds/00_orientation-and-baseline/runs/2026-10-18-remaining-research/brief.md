## Objective and boundaries

Deliver decision-ready recommendations, alternatives, confidence, and concrete validation activities. Do not confuse completion of desk research with proof of product demand, usability, legal compliance, or production readiness.

## Method and stopping rule

Read the relevant drafts, instructions and prior findings. Each workstream inspects a focused set of roughly 6–10 strong primary sources (more only for a material gap), then stops when it can compare realistic options and name what would change the recommendation. Prefer a bounded test over repeated searches for unknowable demand or willingness to pay. No exact provider ranking without a comparable benchmark.

Separate source observations, interpretation, recommendations and unknowns. Keep source summaries concise; link supporting sources at the claim. Retain contradictory evidence and blocked access. Record sources once per workstream and reference them from integrated outputs.
