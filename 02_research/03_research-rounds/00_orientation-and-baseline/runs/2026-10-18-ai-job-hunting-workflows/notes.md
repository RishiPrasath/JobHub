# Collection and method notes

## Execution

The user approved the proposed research run in this conversation. The coordinator wrote the brief, then dispatched three independent read-only collection agents: Zeno (practices), Locke (tools), and Boyle (Singapore domain). Dalton provided a separate UX interpretation after internal evidence was available. The coordinator inspected the supplied internal workspace and relevant linked process documents, recorded sanitized observations, and integrated the handoffs.

## Search approach

Behavior lane: original studies of writing assistance and cover letters, method-described candidate surveys, disability/accessibility research, and two public first-person workflow accounts. Tool lane: current official project/context documentation plus a purposive Teal/Huntr/Simplify sample. Singapore lane: MOM/WSG statistical and programme material, NTUC service walkthrough, local expert guidance, and recruiting-industry surveys. This is a bounded exploratory scan, not a systematic review or exhaustive competitor census.

The coordinator's supplementary Pew/Indeed search returned mainly workplace adoption material; excluded. A PDPC source could not be read due to verification protection; recorded as a gap. No bypass or private credential use attempted.

## Observation versus interpretation

- Directly observed here: source/document text and internal process structure.
- Source-reported: study outcomes, survey responses, product entitlements, and first-person accounts.
- Inferred: continuity and correction effort may be useful JobHub differentiators.
- Proposed: prototype workflows, user-test measures, and synthetic benchmark tasks.

## Method adjustment

No controlled comparisons inside the six products were performed. Accessible documentation was compared instead; it cannot establish output quality, runtime behavior, or actual compatibility. A [synthetic fixture](../../../../05_evidence/datasets-and-examples/2026-10-18-synthetic-workflow-check.md) was prepared for later matched testing. No interviews were conducted or participants contacted. These remain validation work rather than completed research activities.

## Contradictions to retain

Nongenerative writing assistance and generative cover-letter tools are different interventions. Survey attitudes, adoption, and employer-side exposure have different denominators. Editing-time correlation is not proof that editing caused success. Local ATS advice differs across sources. Product snippet text can lag living documentation. The personal process is documented intent, not measured effectiveness.
