# Limitations and remaining work

- Initial desk-research scan, not a systematic literature review, population study, or complete research-backlog execution.
- No new interviews, diaries, participant observation, or product usability tests. Published firsthand accounts and qualitative studies are not our own observations.
- No live controlled comparisons across the products. The synthetic fixture is prepared, unexecuted; model ranking and measured time savings are unavailable.
- The internal scan covers seven process/role documents and navigation inventories, not the entire Job Hunt vault. Private profiles, application outcomes, transcripts, and contracts were not inspected. Workflow instructions show intended process, not adherence or success.
- Singapore-specific AI-use estimates are weak; recruitment-vendor surveys have sampling and consistency limits. Do not convert them into national prevalence or employer consensus.
- Global evidence overrepresents English-language professional/digital work and freelance platforms. Local labour-market surveys have their own coverage exclusions. Frontline, non-English, disabled, and low-digital-confidence experiences need further study.
- Publication dates can be recent while underlying behavior is older. P01/P02 and P06 have older underlying evidence; D06 is outside the requested period. P02 has a visible version-date discrepancy. Living documentation may change immediately after access.
- Search snippets, unreadable pages, and form-gated full reports were not treated as inspected evidence. See sources.md for access gaps. No legal/compliance determination was completed.
- Official capability/pricing/policy descriptions are vendor claims about their service, not independent runtime or privacy audits. Dollar currency uncertainty for Huntr is retained. Product absence was not inferred from a small documentation sample.
- Surveys measuring acceptance, personal use, exposure to employer AI, and hypothetical withdrawal cannot be pooled. No causal employment claim follows from testimonials, editing correlations, or higher application volume.
- Mobile preference, cross-device abandonment, willingness to pay, and durable competitive advantage remain unresolved.

## Stopping assessment

Enough evidence has been collected to compare candidate experiments and identify clear validation gaps. It is not sufficient to finalize product scope, platform, stack, or automation. Further broad desk research has lower immediate value than direct workflow observation and a small prototype comparison; specific blocked sources can be revisited where they affect a decision.
