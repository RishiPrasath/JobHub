# UX and User-Flow Designer handoff

Specialist: Dalton. Method: read-only comparison of repository UX strategy with sanitized I01-I07 internal evidence. No user testing performed.

## Documented flow

Intake → source/fit screening → owner chooses a role → research and strategy → resume/cover-letter preparation → validation and review → confirmed manual submission. Interview preparation reuses application evidence and records practice/debriefs.

## Candidate experiments

| Experiment | Observe | Proposed pilot success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screen one uncertain role | Whether a user distinguishes facts, inferences, and missing requirements and chooses save/defer/skip | Four of five participants complete unaided within five minutes; all understand that screening does not submit an application |
| Review one application draft | Detect a seeded unsupported claim and export failure | Four of five find both without hints; no participant approves the unsupported claim |
| Resume interrupted preparation | Reconstruct state and next action after a device change | Four of five recover within sixty seconds; no lost edits, duplicate capture, or false submitted status |

These thresholds are design proposals, not empirical benchmarks or statistical validation. Begin with a small diverse pilot, then revise tasks and targets based on observed difficulties.

Responsible AI review correction: record each participant's failures and accommodation needs. Four successful participants cannot cancel out a fifth person's exclusion. Treat timings as descriptive; investigate access barriers regardless of aggregate completion. This addendum is the coordinator's response to review, not an assertion of user-test results.

## Unresolved questions

Where do interruptions actually happen? What belongs on a phone? Does cross-device transfer save time? Does source inspection overwhelm users? Which checks can be automated without hiding important choices? No inspected document answers these behavioral questions.

## Accessibility review plan

Use the same tasks with keyboard navigation, screen readers, enlarged text, narrow layouts, and touch. Check focus, names, error/status announcements, plain-language explanations, and recovery without losing work. A task-blocking defect fails the tested scenario; passing these scenarios would not constitute accessibility certification.

## Recommendation and confidence

Start by testing role screening and evidence comprehension, then interrupted return and truthful drafting. High confidence in the documented process; low confidence in inferred usability and prevalence. Keep platform choice open. Coordinator will reconcile this recommendation with external evidence before proposing a first product experiment.
