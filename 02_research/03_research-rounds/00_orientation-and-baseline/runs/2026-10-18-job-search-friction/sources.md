## Independence and quality controls

- D02 and D04 overlap in their historical caregiver series. G01 shares the MOM family; do not treat these as independent replications. D01, D03 and D05 use different instruments but still share institutional provenance.
- D06's release and any university/news repetition form one survey family. Its separate administrative comparison uses a different measure, not a second workflow study.
- P01 and P02 are independent data collections. P02 and P03 have distinct reporting but a shared publisher. P04 is another publisher with an illustrative case. Neither case volume nor publisher count establishes population prevalence.
- P05 is a commissioned programme evaluation with selection toward supported/successful employment. G02 is independent academic/community research. Both disclose methods but cover different populations, so agreement supports a mechanism question rather than a common rate.
- P05's exact release date remains unknown. Its older data are explicitly permitted under the approved relevance exception; no current policy claim is drawn from it.
- Source publication is not the observation date. All confidence judgments must retain underlying periods and population boundaries.
- Full-text text extraction was used for retained sources. No chart-only claim was inferred without readable supporting text. This run did not perform visual PDF layout QA or a statistical reanalysis.

There were no retained post-cutoff sources.

Search scope is purposive and bounded, not systematic or exhaustive. Source count is not a quality score. Revisit source currency before a consequential product decision or on 2027-01-18, whichever comes first; this is a review date, not a scheduled automation.
