# Round 07 — Competitive landscape

## Purpose

Understand how competing job-search, profile, matching, resume and AI career products work from a job seeker’s perspective.

## Core questions

1. How do products capture or build a candidate profile?
2. What information do they use to match or recommend jobs?
3. What is the user-facing process from onboarding to recommendation or application preparation?
4. What do they charge, and what is included in free versus paid access?
5. What are the practical strengths, weaknesses, limitations and trade-offs?
6. What does JobHub need to understand before choosing a differentiated direction?

## Evidence boundary

This round uses public sources only. Marketing pages are recorded as company claims; observed workflows, pricing pages, help documentation and user-visible product behavior are kept separate. Pricing and availability are dated because they can change quickly.

## Deliverables

- `round-brief.md` — research scope and agent assignments
- `comparison-matrix.md` — normalized competitor comparison
- `source-register.md` — source-level provenance and dates
- `round-synthesis.md` — findings, gaps and implications for JobHub
- `limitations.md` — what could not be verified
- `run-log.md` — specialist assignments, evidence handling and validation record
