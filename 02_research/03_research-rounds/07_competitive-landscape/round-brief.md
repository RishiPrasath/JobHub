# Round 07 brief — Competitive landscape

Date started: 2026-10-28

## Research objective

Build an evidence-backed view of how existing products capture candidate information, match people to jobs, support application preparation and charge for those capabilities.

## Scope

Cover representative products across four groups:

1. Job boards and professional networks.
2. AI-first job-search and job-recommendation products.
3. Resume, ATS-matching and application-workflow tools.
4. Career profiles, coaching, interview and adjacent tools where they affect the same workflow.

## Required fields per competitor

- Product and audience
- Primary workflow
- Profile-capture method
- Profile fields or signals used
- Matching or recommendation process
- Application-preparation capabilities
- Automation level and human-control points
- Free access and paid pricing
- Pricing date and region/currency
- Privacy and data-use signals
- Strengths
- Weaknesses and failure modes
- Platform or access limitations
- Directly observed facts versus company claims
- Sources and unresolved questions

## Non-goals

- Do not copy competitor features into JobHub scope.
- Do not infer internal algorithms from marketing language.
- Do not treat one user review as representative evidence.
- Do not conduct logins, paid subscriptions, applications or external writes.
- Do not make a final product or pricing decision in this round.

## Agent assignments

### Competitive Landscape Researcher — job boards and networks

Study LinkedIn, Indeed, MyCareersFuture and comparable job-board workflows. Focus on profile capture, matching signals, job alerts, application flow, free/paid access and limitations.

### AI Tools Expert — AI-first job search

Study current AI-first job discovery and career products such as Jobright, Teal, Careerflow, Sonara or comparable products that are publicly accessible. Verify current positioning, onboarding, matching, automation, pricing and provider claims.

### Job-Seeker Practices / application tools researcher — resume and application layer

Study Jobscan, Rezi, Kickresume, Huntr, Simplify, Careerflow or comparable tools. Focus on resume/profile ingestion, ATS matching, tailoring, tracking, interview support, pricing and user-control trade-offs.

### Evidence Synthesizer — cross-cutting reviewer

Inspect the current JobHub research context and independently review selected official sources. Define comparison categories, identify evidence quality problems, reconcile conflicts and list what remains unknown. Do not decide JobHub scope.

## Handoff format

Each specialist should return:

- task and scope;
- products and sources inspected;
- observed facts;
- source-reported claims;
- pricing and access date;
- strengths and weaknesses;
- uncertainty and limitations;
- implications worth considering;
- no files changed.
