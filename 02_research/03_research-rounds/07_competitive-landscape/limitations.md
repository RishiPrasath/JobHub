# Round 07 limitations and unresolved questions

Research date: 2026-10-28

## Method limitations

- Research used public webpages, help centres, pricing pages, privacy policies, terms and selected independent status/review pages.
- No competitor accounts were created.
- No paid plans or trials were purchased.
- No browser extensions were installed.
- No applications were submitted.
- No authenticated workflow was independently tested.
- “Observed workflow” means publicly documented workflow, not proof that every user sees the same interface.
- No independent causal evidence was found that ATS scores, AI tailoring or automatic application volume improves hiring outcomes.

## Pricing limitations

- Prices are mostly USD and may not represent Singapore prices.
- Weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-year, annual and lifetime plans are not directly comparable.
- Promotional pricing, app-store pricing, taxes and renewal terms may differ.
- Jobscan checkout was not verified from the accessible public page.
- Jobright consumer pricing was not verified; an employer price was exposed instead.
- Sonara consumer pricing was not clearly displayed.
- Careerflow pages showed conflicting public pricing figures.

## Regional limitations

- Singapore-specific support was confirmed most clearly for MyCareersFuture and Jobstreet.
- Simplify’s documented matching-country list did not include Singapore.
- Careerflow’s documented Job Portal was U.S.-only.
- Sonara signup exposed U.S. states.
- Jobright public evidence was strongly U.S.-oriented.
- Teal’s Singapore coverage was not confirmed.
- The reviewed sources did not establish local PDPA handling, Singapore salary norms or MyCareersFuture integration for most AI-first products.

## Product and evidence contradictions

1. Jobright public pages disagree about whether the agent submits applications autonomously or requires user review.
2. Jobright’s `jobright.ai` and `jobright.live` pages publish materially different privacy and portability language.
3. MyCareersFuture documentation uses different descriptions of skills matching across pages, which may reflect product evolution.
4. Careerflow public feature and pricing pages are not fully consistent.
5. Huntr feature pages are current while its privacy policy and terms are older.
6. Indeed and Jobstreet document employer visibility and third-party copies, but the duration and practical removal of copies remain unclear.
7. Vendor outcome claims are not independently validated in this round.

## Questions requiring direct validation later

- How accurate is profile/resume parsing for tables, columns, unusual career paths and cross-industry experience?
- How often do recommendations include stale, duplicate, already-filled or unsuitable jobs?
- What happens when a user changes a preference or corrects a profile field?
- Can the user inspect which profile facts caused a recommendation?
- Can every generated resume claim be traced to candidate evidence?
- What is the real status after an external ATS submission?
- How are employer copies, recruiter discovery and third-party AI processing controlled?
- Does the user understand the difference between a keyword score, a fit signal and a genuine suitability judgment?
- Which workflows matter to Singapore users outside professional technology roles?
