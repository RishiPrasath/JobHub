## Disagreements between sources
- Scam-loss reduction in 2025: SPF's page gives about 18%; Malay Mail reports 19% in a search summary. SPF used.
- Job-scam rank in 2025: SPF's page says third by total losses; a search summary of the brief said third by case count with 14.9% of cases. Different measures, the second unverified.
- EP appeal time: MOM's EP page says 85% within 6 weeks; other passes quote 3 weeks and at least 8 weeks. Different passes, not a conflict.
- EP salary floor: the overview page shows $5,600 as the minimum; the eligibility page shows the age-based range up to $10,700 and the 1 Jan 2027 step-up.
