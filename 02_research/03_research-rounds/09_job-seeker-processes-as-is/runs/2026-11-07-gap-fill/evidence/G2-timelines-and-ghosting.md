## Disagreements between sources
- Time to hire: Employment Hero (2023) gives 41 to 42 days; Randstad (2017) about 68 days, and a MyCareersFuture article repeats the 68 figure as if current. Different samples, years and measures (employers versus job seekers); not reconcilable.
- Ghosting figures: Robert Walters' 88% comes from a self-selected poll; a snippet of its own release showed 88% plus 13%, which exceeds 100%. NTU's reprint does not mention the 13%. Unresolved; the 88% is weak.
- Graduate median salary: one report gives $6,000 to $6,275 (full-time permanent), another $4,317 to $4,500; they appear to measure different groups. No salary question was asked; flagged only.
- Drop-out threshold: Indeed 2026 says many drop out after 2 to 4 weeks; Randstad 2017 says 89% would reject after more than four weeks. Consistent in direction, different in measure.
