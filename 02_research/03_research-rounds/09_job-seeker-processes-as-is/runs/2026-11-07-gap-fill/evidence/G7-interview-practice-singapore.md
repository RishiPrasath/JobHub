## Disagreements between sources
- Consent for reference checks: vendor templates (for example Genie AI, Omni HR) say written consent is required; the PDPC guidelines say a reference from a former employer needs no consent. The PDPC text also says employers must notify candidates and that the exception does not override other laws. Vendors may be describing best practice rather than the legal minimum.
- Duration of government hiring: SCDF's official answer says 2 to 3 months; candidate reviews range from 4 weeks to about 6 months; GRA gives two 2-week response windows with no total.
- Feedback: the Minister says employers should be encouraged to give feedback voluntarily; the 2018 survey says most job seekers get none. Both can hold at once; no data on current practice.
