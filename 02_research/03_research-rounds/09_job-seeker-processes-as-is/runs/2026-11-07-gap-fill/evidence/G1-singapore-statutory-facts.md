## Disagreements between sources
- WFA small-firm exemption: a search summary says firms with fewer than 25 employees are exempt initially. The 4 Nov 2025 press release and second-reading speech, both opened, do not mention it. Treat it as unconfirmed.
- WFA timeline: the speech says the Act was earlier expected in 2026 or 2027 and is now targeted for end-2027. Commercial blogs gave inconsistent passage dates. MOM pages trusted.
- FCF exemptions: a 2013 and a 2020 factsheet in search results show other thresholds (25 employees, $20,000 salary); not opened. The current MOM EP page (Jan 2026) lists fewer than 10 employees and $22,500 and is the one to use.
- CPF rates: the 2026 table and the 2027 increase for senior workers are both current, with different effective dates; any story on older workers should state the year.
