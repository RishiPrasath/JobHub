# Limitations

## How the research was done
- Four Haiku-model agents did the first pass, each on one process, using web search and page reading. They are fast and cheap but make more errors than a larger model. Their notes were treated as leads.
- The lead researcher did not re-open primary sources in this round. Evidence tags in findings.md (S, Sn, A, I) follow the agents' own labelling of what they opened and what they only saw in search snippets.
- Pages were read through a tool that can return a model-written summary, not the page text.

## Evidence quality
- **Mostly snippets and secondary write-ups.** Only a few official pages were opened: MOM parliamentary answers (23 Sep 2025, 4 Mar 2026, 9 Sep 2026), MOM's Employment Pass eligibility page, a TAFEP article, and a CIPD summary of Randstad Singapore research. Everything else, including most survey percentages, was seen as a search snippet.
- **Mostly old, US or vendor data.** Channel-use figures are from 2015 and 2016. Negotiation and counteroffer figures are US or UK and from 2013 to 2020. The 68-day Singapore hiring average is from 2017. Several survey figures come from vendors with a commercial interest and small or convenience samples (a Singapore graduate poll had 105 respondents and is not representative).
- **Interview rounds in Singapore come from Glassdoor candidate reports**, which are anecdotal and not representative.
- **Pages that did not load:** the MyCareersFuture FAQ PDF, the Monster application reports, Pew's 2015 job-seeking page, several MOM, CPF and TAFEP statutory pages, and a LifeSG job-search guide.

## Not verified (do not rely on without checking the official page)
- MyCareersFuture application steps, filter names and eligibility wording.
- Notice-period bands under the Employment Act (secondary sources only), the Key Employment Terms content, CPF contribution rates, and Employment Pass salary thresholds. The MOM page states some figures for 2027, which is after today.
- Background check coverage and turnaround (vendor blogs).
- Whether the Fair Consideration Framework advertising rule makes some postings not actively open to local candidates. That is an inference, not a stated finding.

## Gaps (nothing found)
- How people track applications.
- Typical time to response, and drop-off by stage, in Singapore.
- Current Singapore job-search duration.
- Foreign professionals' job-search behaviour, and low-wage workers' channels, in Singapore.
- Government, multinational and small-company differences in interview practice.
- Singapore negotiation rates and typical pay uplift; counteroffer outcomes.
