## Main findings

- Preparation burden includes checking, scheduling, repeated entry and uncertainty. Reusable examples and human/manual support supply alternatives to rewriting everything.
- Fit needs actual tasks, personal conditions and demonstrated evidence. Missing information, trainable gaps and verified essential requirements should remain distinguishable.
- Red flags and employer language are reasons to verify specific concerns; they cannot establish intent or misconduct by themselves.
- Pay comparison needs comparable roles, dated sources and component definitions. Negotiation depends on the opportunity and constraints; no universal salary target or safe uplift is established.
- Truthful tailoring and interview practice should help the person select, explain and rehearse real experience. AI adds checking and export/review work whose cost belongs in evaluation.
- Internal procedures offer useful truthful-claim, trust/fit, confirmation and practice patterns, with observed documentation inconsistencies, technical-career assumptions, rigid advice and unverified current operation.
- A reusable evidence record raises disclosure, access and retention questions. Current provider handling and privacy controls remain unreviewed.
- Video advice adds concrete methods and counterexamples; weak tutorials, old international salary advice and unverified statistics are explicitly bounded.

See F01–F12 in the run findings for source routes and confidence. Local accessibility and cover-letter behaviour, exact phone-to-laptop loss, current process effectiveness and JobHub demand/benefit remain unresolved.
