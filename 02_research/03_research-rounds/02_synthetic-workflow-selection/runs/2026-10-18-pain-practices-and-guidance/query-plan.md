# Executed query plan

Prepared before retrieval: the Research Query Strategist's [nine-family portfolio](../../query-plan-draft.md) is adopted for this run under the [run brief](brief.md). The coordinator acts as Research Planner. This file records scope and handoff adaptations; exact executed queries belong in the query log and specialist reports.

Prefer 2023–2026 material; older foundational advice must be dated and labelled. Singapore-first with labelled international comparison; cross-industry intent. Record occupations actually represented and gaps. Use public English sources; treat missing languages and inaccessible captions as retrieval limitations.

## Search venues and adapters

Use native web search, YouTube discovery through indexed public results, and subtitle metadata/caption extraction through existing free `yt-dlp`. Official venues include MOM, WSG/MyCareersFuture, TAFEP, ScamShield; primary papers are research evidence and career services provide attributed guidance. Public forums are bounded self-reports. Quoted phrases and `site:` are adapters; record exact syntax used. Do not expose private workspace phrases or identifiers to external search.

## Sequence and budget

1. Read prepared plan and five supplied transcript captures.
2. Public specialists execute assigned independent families; internal reviewer scans process material separately.
3. Video specialist screens up to eight new candidates across gaps and saves useful English captions only.
4. Coordinator assesses gaps, performs a small primary-study follow-up if needed, and synthesizes.
5. Independent evidence/quality review checks objective coverage, sourcing, overclaims, privacy, contradictions, and recommendation bounds.

Three initial queries per family before retrieval-quality review; log follow-ups and reasons. Video cap is eight screened additional candidates, not a quota. Two independent source families and a contrary/alternative case are the target for strong themes. Stop with explicit gaps when retrieval/access fails or new searches add duplication. Do not claim saturation or representativeness.

## Source and query records

Record query ID/family, exact query, tool/venue, date, useful IDs, access failure/redundancy, and next disposition. Record source ID, title, stable URL/path, creator/publisher, publication and access date, evidence type, geography/population, disclosed method, family, inspected section/timestamp, and limits. Keep firsthand, advice, study, official context, private process, and synthetic judgments identifiable. Discovery-only pages and unexecuted planned queries do not become evidence.

## Retrieval risks

Career-coach/SEO content and dramatic negative stories may dominate; one creator or repeated survey is one family. English and tech/office-worker coverage may omit frontline or low-digital-confidence contexts. Older salary evidence does not estimate today's individual value. Search frequency does not measure prevalence. Red-flag language and supplied-video selection may bias interpretation; seek questions, explanations, and successful alternatives. Auto-caption text can distort negations and numbers; no claim of listening unless performed. Internal process documents describe one owner's intended practices and cannot establish general demand.
