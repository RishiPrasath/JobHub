2. Investigate job/employer red flags, role fit, transferable skills, missing qualifications, and how to verify concerns.
3. Investigate pay research, market value, full compensation, and negotiation practices with geography and date stated.
4. Investigate truthful resume tailoring and interview preparation without invented experience.
5. Investigate actual AI use, checking, privacy, automation trade-offs, and successful manual alternatives.
6. Investigate interruption, return, accessibility, and device context without assuming a mobile/web platform.
7. Critically examine employer language and alternative explanations for hiring difficulty.
8. Review all five supplied English caption captures; discover up to eight additional candidate videos and retrieve useful English captions for free where available.
9. Scan the private Job Hunt workspace and relevant linked agent/workflow material for documented strengths, weaknesses, reusable practices, and gaps.
10. Produce traceable findings, contradictions, confidence, a coverage assessment, and proposed next research/product steps.
