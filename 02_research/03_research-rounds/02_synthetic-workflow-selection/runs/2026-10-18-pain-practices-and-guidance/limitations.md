# Limits of this Round 02 pass

Date: 2026-10-18. These limits accompany every finding and recommendation.

- Public convenience material and one private process are not a representative sample. No direct participants, employer verification, product usability evaluation, provider comparison, or current workflow execution occurred.
- Advice describes recommendations, not demonstrated behaviour or efficacy. Public outcomes and forum identities remain unverified; relative dates can conflict. Research reports have different methods and transfer limits.
- Singapore sources are present, but graduate/office/tech perspectives and English material remain prominent. International access/AI/negotiation studies do not establish local prevalence. Frontline, multilingual and accessibility contexts remain incompletely covered.
- Repeated NUS/WSG/MOM/Reddit/creator families are not independent replications. Record counts must not become evidence-strength scores or a prevalence estimate.
- Compensation data have reference dates, population exclusions and distinct basic/gross/variable definitions. This pass does not estimate any individual's salary value, negotiability, offer-withdrawal risk, legal eligibility or employment-rights outcome.
- Employer phrases, vague duties, skills shortages and poor conditions can have multiple explanations. No detector accuracy, individual misconduct, scam prevalence or employer-intent inference is established.
- The exact Singapore phone discovery → deferred laptop application → lost opportunity episode remains unsubstantiated. Broader interrupted work, saving failures and access constraints cannot be joined into an invented episode.
- Information overload, privacy practice, truthful cover-letter checking, and low-digital-confidence recovery have thinner behavioural evidence. This run conducted no original timing or conversion tests. Included studies report bounded outcomes; no JobHub benefit, Singapore end-to-end net-effort effect, abandonment or willingness-to-pay estimate is established.
- Subtitle review is text analysis. Auto-captions may distort numbers, negations and names; manual captions also are not proof that advice is correct. Key audiovisual passages were not independently listened to in this run unless the video report explicitly states otherwise. No unsupported video statistic is promoted.
- Internal review sampled 25 process documents and five historical artifacts. Private resumes, contracts, correspondence and outcomes were excluded. Documentation mismatches are observed; live operational impact and coordination burden are not measured. July artifacts predate later document changes.
- Search/access failures and excluded candidates are logged. Some results were batched, preventing exact per-query ranking reconstruction. No search-time measurement, exhaustive retrieval or saturation claim is made.
- The historical synthetic comparison predates this expanded pass; it cannot establish user need. New evidence does not retrospectively validate its walkthroughs or select a product workflow.

Completion here means the authorised bounded activities were carried out, findings/gaps documented, and independent review addressed. It does not mean all underlying uncertainties are resolved. Proposed next experiments remain subject to owner choice and must retain a manual comparison and realistic counterexamples.
