# Research review rubric

Defined before integrated findings. This rubric evaluates this research record, not JobHub usability.

| Criterion | Sufficient for this bounded pass | Failure requiring correction |
|---|---|---|
| Objective coverage | All ten objectives map to inspected material, a finding, and an explicit evidence/access gap where needed | Objective silently omitted or claimed resolved without evidence |
| Source grounding | Claims cite opened pages or exact caption/file sections; source type/scope/date are visible | Snippet, uninspected page, or agent assertion treated as evidence |
| Independence | Repeated creators, surveys, posts, and publisher families identified | Repetition presented as independent corroboration or prevalence |
| Firsthand pain versus advice | Public experience, study, official guidance, and advice remain distinguishable | Advice paraphrased as observed job-seeker behaviour |
| Alternatives | Positive cases, clarifications, manual workarounds, and alternative explanations retained | Only confirming red-flag or product-need material used |
| Honest application help | Guidance uses verified candidate facts, identifies actual gaps, and permits declining a role | Invented metrics/qualifications, misleading fit scores, or deception endorsed |
| Pay and job quality | Salary scope/date/definition and negotiability limits recorded | One figure or phrase establishes personal worth, bad employer, or universal rule |
| Internal process | Bounded safe inventory, strengths/weaknesses, intended versus observed execution | Personal data copied, process plans claimed operational, or one owner generalised |
| Video method | Five supplied captures reviewed; additional-candidate cap respected; metadata/captions/timestamps checked | Captions claimed audio-verified, sources fabricated, or blocked video claimed analysed |
| Research/product boundary | Recommendations and remaining uncertainties explicit; no platform/stack/scope acceptance | Research findings silently become final product requirements |

Reviewer records actual inspected files and any unverified assertions. Completion requires correcting material failures and stating residual limits; it does not require proving user demand, saturation, or market prevalence.
