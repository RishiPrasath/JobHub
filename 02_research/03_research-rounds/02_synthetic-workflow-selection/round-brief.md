## Research objectives

The owner wants to better understand what people say about the pains of job hunting, applications, and interview preparation. The [current run brief](runs/2026-10-18-pain-practices-and-guidance/brief.md) defines ten coverage objectives:

1. Reported search, application, resume/cover-letter and interview pains, repeated preparation, uncertainty, time pressure, and overload.
2. Job/employer red flags, role fit, transferable skills, missing qualifications, and verification questions.
3. Knowing one's market value, salary research, full compensation, and negotiation.
4. Truthful tailoring and interview preparation using real experience.
5. Day-to-day AI use, checking, privacy, automation trade-offs, and manual alternatives.
6. Posting capture, interruptions, return, accessibility, and device context.
7. Employer language and job quality, with alternative explanations.
8. All five supplied videos plus bounded discovery of additional English-captioned videos.
9. Internal Job Hunt workspace scan for strengths, weaknesses, reusable practices, and gaps.
10. Traceable synthesis, contradictions, limitations, and proposed next steps.
