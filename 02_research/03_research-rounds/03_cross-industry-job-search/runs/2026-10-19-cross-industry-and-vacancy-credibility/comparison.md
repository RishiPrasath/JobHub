## Common information, conditional interpretation

| Shared question | Why the answer must stay contextual |
| --- | --- |
| What work is this? | A title can hide sales duties, client deployment, specialised care or an actual production task. |
| Can I meet the requirements? | A regulated qualification, preferred certificate, transferable skill and portfolio sample are different evidence. |
| Can I do it under these conditions? | Hours, shifts, travel, site, training commitments and arrangement can change feasibility. |
| What must I prepare? | A service example, process explanation, portfolio or technical quiz are not interchangeable preparation tasks. |
| What will I earn? | Basic, gross, allowances, variable incentives and project fees need separate units and conditions. |
| Is recruitment active? | Visibility, authenticity, external activity, outcome and intent remain separate; unknown is valid. |
| Does assistance help? | AI claims, human guidance and manual alternatives need comparison; no JobHub benefit was measured. |

## Outside a product's demonstrated control

This corpus does not show that software can create approved headcount, shorten an employer's checks, award professional credentials, improve transport access or compel payment/response. It may help formulate questions or organise evidence, but that is a hypothesis to test, not an implemented or accepted capability.
