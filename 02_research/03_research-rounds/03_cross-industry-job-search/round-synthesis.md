## Main findings

- Industry labels alone hide important role, arrangement and entry-route differences. Six deep dives cover food/hospitality, healthcare/social care, logistics/transport, manufacturing/engineering/trades, finance/insurance and creative/media/arts. Seven further profiles remain breadth-only.
- Practical feasibility, professional eligibility, transferable experience and truthful evidence need separate treatment. Interview preparation can involve actual service examples, clinical eligibility, technical reasoning, portfolios or performance samples rather than only application prose.
- Compensation needs context: basic or gross pay, conditional allowances, variable incentives and project fees are not equivalent. This corpus does not establish representative pay or negotiation outcomes.
- Fake/ghost posting concerns have a documented local historical basis, but stale/duplicated ads, disclosed future pools, changed circumstances and impersonation are different phenomena. Neither silence nor reposting alone establishes deliberate deception.
- Hiring authenticity, visible status, active external recruitment, eventual outcome and contemporaneous intent remain separate. Original surveys, applicant suspicions, non-fill data and classifiers do not establish a current Singapore deceptive-advert rate.
- AI application practices are reported in a generic local discussion, with uncertain occupations and unverified outcomes. Advice, prototypes and missing mentions cannot stand in for demonstrated use or non-use in each sector. Manual and human alternatives remain relevant comparators.

These are source-attributed findings and coordinator interpretations, not validated product benefits. All consequential qualifications and original sources are in the dated run.

## What remains unresolved

Recent complete operational/trades and warehouse/driver journeys, arts/project outcomes, sector-specific AI behaviour, explicit non-use, accessibility/multilingual contexts, negotiation outcomes and current requisition-level intent remain thin or unanswered. Several positive transitions are historical or selected by employers/providers. Public accounts are self-selected and frequently share a platform.

The 66 queries, 140 inspection attempts, 86 retained source IDs/URLs and 84 observations are accounting measures, not independent participants or proof of saturation. Reused IDs and source-family dependence are retained. No direct user access or private-drive rescan occurred.
