## Research results

Start with the [dated run](runs/2026-10-19-cross-industry-and-vacancy-credibility/README.md), [findings](runs/2026-10-19-cross-industry-and-vacancy-credibility/findings.md) and [13 industry profiles](runs/2026-10-19-cross-industry-and-vacancy-credibility/industry-profiles/README.md). Six non-IT deep dives, a vacancy-credibility report, source/query registers and explicit gaps accompany them. Collection closed at 66 searches and 140 inspection attempts; those counts are not independent evidence or representative coverage.

This round asks what job seeking involves in different industries and occupations: the distinctive pressures, hiring routes, requirements, preparation, interviews, conditions, pay, and uncertainty people describe. It will compare similarities and differences without stereotyping a sector or selecting JobHub's product scope.

The design is Singapore-first and public-web-only because the owner has no direct user access. A broad scan of 13 research baskets precedes deeper comparison of six contrasting baskets. IT is a comparator, not the default; at least five deep dives must concern non-IT baskets. The selection is a research sample, not a target-market decision.

Fake/ghost postings form a separate strand across that comparison. Distinguish employer-reported or corroborated hiring intent from job-seeker suspicions, stale listings, duplicates, talent pools, freezes, cancelled roles and external scams. No response or a long-lived ad alone proves none of those explanations.
