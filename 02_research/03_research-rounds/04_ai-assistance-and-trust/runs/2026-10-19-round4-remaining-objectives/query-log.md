# Query and coverage log

## Planning record

The Query Strategist created a portable vocabulary map and query families before specialist research. Exact provider, geography, population, date, source family and duplicate-family checks were required for each result. The specialists reported findings and source links; this log records the bounded coverage rather than inventing a search-count total.

## Query families used

| Family | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Singapore sector structure | Skills Frameworks, SFA, EMA, PUB, NEA, MPA, MOM and SSOC material |
| Occupational fit and credentials | role tasks, licences, safety, medical fitness, STCW/CoC, WSQ, training and work conditions |
| Market value | MOM occupational wages, PWM, progression, demand and employer/sector pathways |
| Truthful tailoring | transferable skills, career changes, gaps, caregiving, freelance/contract work, metrics and ownership language |
| Formatting/ATS | document structure, columns, tables, graphics, PDF/DOCX, parsing and accessibility |
| AI job seeking | discovery, summarisation, resumes, cover letters, interviews, tracking, autofill and browser agents |
| Trust and safety | privacy, retention, provenance, bias, homogenisation, prompt injection, review and portability |
| Disconfirmation | mixed outcome studies, lower/higher adoption estimates, worker-experience tensions and vendor limitations |

## Coverage check

- Five run objectives addressed.
- Four industry/employment contexts profiled beyond IT.
- Official Singapore sources, research, guidance, vendor documentation and public user reports were kept distinct.
- Supporting and disconfirming evidence was sought for AI outcomes, adoption, ATS advice and worker experience.
- The stopping rule was met for a bounded desk run: each objective has multiple source families, remaining uncertainty is recorded, and further browsing would mainly expand rather than resolve the largest gaps.
