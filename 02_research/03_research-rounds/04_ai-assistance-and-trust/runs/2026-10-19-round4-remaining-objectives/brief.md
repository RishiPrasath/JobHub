# Round 04 remaining-objectives research brief

Status: complete — bounded public-web run completed 2026-10-19

Date: 2026-10-19 (Singapore time)

## Decision context

How might JobHub help people prepare truthfully and intelligently for suitable roles across industries, while keeping recommendations understandable, accessible, reviewable and under the job seeker’s control?

## Scope of this run

This run covers the Round 04 objectives not addressed by the completed job-posting page inventory:

1. extend industry and employment-context coverage beyond the existing Round 03 profiles;
2. research truthful role-specific resume/CV tailoring;
3. research content, formatting, ATS-readability and accessibility trade-offs;
4. research how people identify suitable roles and form a grounded view of market value;
5. examine AI-assisted job-seeking practices, review behaviour, trust, privacy and human-control needs.

## Geography and audience

- Singapore-first public-web evidence;
- international evidence only when geography and transfer limits are explicit;
- job seekers who are unemployed, underemployed, changing careers, retrenched, inexperienced, returning to work, time-constrained or unfamiliar with advanced AI workflows;
- cross-industry coverage, keeping industry, occupation, seniority and employment arrangement separate.

## Explicit exclusions

- no participant recruitment, contact or profiling;
- no private-group or private-workspace extraction;
- no personal resumes, identifiable applicant data or external submissions;
- no product-scope, platform, stack or AI-provider decision;
- no promise that a resume format, role-fit method or AI tool guarantees an interview or job;
- no population-prevalence claim from anecdotes, vendor marketing or convenience sources.

## Evidence requirements

Prefer official Singapore labour and career guidance, occupational/professional bodies, credible research, transparent surveys, employer/recruiter guidance with incentives identified, public job-seeker accounts, and dated tool documentation. Record the source population, geography, date, method, ownership and limitations. Preserve direct observations separately from interpretation.

## Required outputs

- additional-industry evidence matrix and profiles;
- truthful resume/CV tailoring evidence matrix;
- formatting, ATS-readability and accessibility trade-off matrix;
- role-fit and market-value evidence matrix;
- AI job-seeker workflow and trust/risk matrix;
- integrated findings, open questions, and a human decision gate for JobHub.

## Stopping rule

Stop when each objective has evidence from more than one source family, positive and disconfirming cases have been checked, new searches mainly repeat known concepts, and remaining uncertainty is explicitly attributable to weak public evidence or the need for participant research. Do not use additional browsing to manufacture certainty.
