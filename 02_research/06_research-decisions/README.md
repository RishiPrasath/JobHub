# Research Decisions

This folder contains decisions that arise directly from research and may affect JobHub direction.

Use `YYYY-MM-DD-decision-slug.md` and include:

- Decision and status
- Research question
- Evidence and linked results
- Options considered
- Rationale
- Consequences and risks
- Confidence
- Revisit trigger
- Human owner

Durable product or technical decisions must also be reflected in [`01_strategy/08_decision-log.md`](../../01_strategy/08_decision-log.md).
