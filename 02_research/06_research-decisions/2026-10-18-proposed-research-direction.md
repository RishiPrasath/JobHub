# Proposed direction after desk research

Date: 2026-10-18. **Status: proposed; no human acceptance recorded.** Owner: JobHub product owner. These are choices to review individually, not an instruction to approve the whole package.

Research questions: R-01–R-15. Evidence: [cross-research synthesis](../04_results/00_cross-research-synthesis.md), [coverage](../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-remaining-research/coverage.md), [review record](../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-remaining-research/review.md).

| Proposal | Context, alternatives and rationale | Consequence / confidence / revisit trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P01: pause broad desk collection and run public user-insight research | Compare adjacent-role preparation and frontline condition comparison using public firsthand accounts, reviews, surveys and qualitative studies; alternatives are more desk research, existing tools or no product. Direct user access is unavailable, so public evidence is the current feasible input | Requires the [web-based user-insight brief](../03_research-rounds/01_public-web-user-insights/runs/2026-10-18-web-based-user-insights/brief.md) and its source-quality limits. Moderate process confidence, low demand confidence. Revisit if independent public evidence does not support either hypothesis |
| P02: keep platform selection open through flow comparison | Compare responsive and capture-companion concepts; desktop-only, native, PWA and extension remain options. Public opinions can inform the hypotheses, but cannot establish device preference or usability | Requires synthetic workflow comparison first and later device testing if authorised. Revisit after a necessary capability fails or public evidence points elsewhere |
| P03: trial a lightweight, owner-explainable hybrid method | Alternatives include spec-heavy, prototype-only or minimal notes/tests. Use only enough detail to protect current risk, with independent acceptance checks | Requires one approved slice and measurable review/rework/understanding. Moderate confidence in rationale, unmeasured local benefit. Revisit after the V05 comparison |
| P04: make evidence and control the design baseline for evaluation | Compare a source/claim contract and transparent uncertainty with simpler manual workflows; defer opaque fit scores and automatic submissions. Truthful preparation is more defensible than implied hiring prediction | Rights, deletion, correction and access controls add work; runtime effectiveness unmeasured. Revisit failed grounding, exclusion, or unsustainable review burden |
| P05: select tools/architecture through bounded tasks | Local, cloud and hybrid remain options; compare deterministic/manual processing with permitted model configurations. Do not choose from popularity alone | Provider/source/privacy review and synthetic benchmark required. Revisit cost, latency, contractual, model or use-case changes |
| P06: keep portable roles and use native Codex coordination | Alternative is a new orchestration framework. Current native delegation is sufficient for this research; custom role registration is a distinct setup change | No `.codex` files created here. Moderate workflow confidence; loading, permissions and benefits need V05a/V05b verification if implemented |

Disagreement/limits: workflow, product and UX specialists focus on different next questions; the coordinator puts problem evidence before surface choice. There is no established strongest segment, universally fastest method or validated commercial advantage. Source preservation and minimisation must be reconciled explicitly, not resolved by keeping everything.

Existing decisions are unchanged: JobHub remains the working name; product surface and technology stack remain open. Public naming overlap and launch/legal/provider constraints remain unresolved. Numerical research targets and retention examples are proposals, not commitments.
