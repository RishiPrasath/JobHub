# Competition, sustainability and naming

Date: 2026-10-18. Reviewed desk synthesis; proposals, not commercial commitments.

The [product/market report](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-remaining-research/product-market.md) extends the [earlier six-tool comparison](../../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-ai-job-hunting-workflows/specialists/ai-tools.md) with job-board intelligence, interview preparation, automated applications and human career-service alternatives. Documentation establishes offerings, not comparative performance, Singapore access or privacy implementation. No durable competitive gap has been proven.

Proposed positioning: help a person understand an opportunity and prepare claims they can substantiate. Test evidence reuse, uncertainty handling and occupation-sensitive conditions against existing tools and a manual baseline. Do not infer defensibility from features competitors can copy.

A useful free/manual path, an optional short preparation pass and institution-supported access are alternatives worth testing. Pricing and partner interest remain unknown. Successful job placement can end product use, so retention alone is not the outcome. Measure total service/correction cost before claiming viability; avoid undisclosed referral incentives or sharing candidate data with sponsors by default.

JobHub remains the chosen working name. Public employment-related uses were observed; no trademark, domain, app-store or handle clearance was completed. The old AI-persona naming panel is not consumer evidence. Singapore-first research does not establish US readiness; local credentials, sources and rights need separate checks.
