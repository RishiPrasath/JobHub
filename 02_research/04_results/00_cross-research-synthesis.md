# JobHub — research synthesis

Updated: 2026-10-18. **Status: desk-research pass complete; reviewed recommendations awaiting human acceptance.** No platform, stack, MVP, pricing or retention policy has been locked in.

## What the research recommends

Use a small, evidence-led development cycle: **understand one real problem → sketch competing flows → agree a short behavior brief → build one approved slice → test and explain it → revise.** This gives specifications a useful role without requiring a detailed specification of the entire application first.

For the product, test a role-by-role workspace for understanding an opportunity, connecting truthful experience and returning to the next action. Compare adjacent-occupation preparation with frontline job-condition comparison. These are hypotheses, not proven demand. Existing tools, a simpler manual workflow and human coaching remain legitimate alternatives to building.

## Findings that matter for decisions

| Area | Conclusion and implication | Confidence / remaining uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User problem | Separate practical working conditions, information uncertainty and process effort. Better software cannot fix all three | Bounded evidence; no ranking of greatest burden or strongest segment |
| AI and competition | General assistants and career tools overlap with the concept. Differentiate through demonstrated usefulness and evidence, not a longer feature list | Documented offerings and limited practice evidence; no superiority or hiring-gain proof |
| Truthfulness and trust | Preserve source versions, confirmed experience, inference, unknowns and corrections; avoid opaque fit scores or unsupported employer warnings | Strong design rationale; comprehension and real accuracy untested |
| Mobile/web | Compare the same interrupted journey across viable surface options; do not assume preparation must move to a laptop | Capability constraints researched; device preference and usability untested |
| Development | Trial lightweight specifications, small changes and independent verification. Add detail for stable/high-consequence behavior | Supported process proposal; local speed and maintenance benefit unmeasured |
| Gherkin/Cucumber | Use selectively where stable examples help agreement and regression testing; ordinary tests plus examples remain an alternative | Mechanism clear; no Cucumber adoption or local trial yet |
| Architecture/models | Compare simple options and a deterministic baseline; measure task quality, corrections, full cost and latency | No stack/model winner; source rights and provider-specific constraints remain gates |
| Codex | Native delegation works in this run; portable role files are not native custom-agent registration. No project `.codex` directory was found | Locally observed; setup/benchmark remains a separate change |
| Owner learning | Personally own user interpretation, acceptance, data-flow understanding, test rationale and rollback; use AI for tutoring and critique | Concrete exercises prepared; mastery not yet demonstrated |

These implications are research proposals, not accepted strategy changes. Detailed source support, contrary evidence and specialist-versus-coordinator distinctions are in the [integrated findings](../03_research-rounds/00_orientation-and-baseline/runs/2026-10-18-remaining-research/findings.md).
