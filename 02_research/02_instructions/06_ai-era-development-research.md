# AI-Era Development Research

## Use this instruction for

Evaluating how JobHub should research, design, implement, test, document, and review software with AI and parallel Codex agents.

## Assigned team

- **Primary:** AI SDLC Advisor.
- **Support:** Technical Architect for engineering trade-offs; Quality and Evaluation Engineer for measurable quality; Prompt Engineer for reusable workflows.
- **Synthesis:** Evidence Synthesizer.
- **Review:** Product Strategist and Decision Record Keeper; add Responsible AI Reviewer when agent permissions or sensitive data are involved.

## Questions to cover

- Where lightweight specifications, Gherkin, tests, prototypes, or exploratory work fit.
- Which SDLC activities are AI-assisted, human-led, or human-only.
- How parallel agents should be bounded, coordinated, reviewed, and evaluated.
- How context, prompts, plans, decisions, and repository instructions remain useful without duplication or staleness.
- How to measure production-qualified value rather than generated output volume.

## Required output

Produce a workflow recommendation, responsibility map, quality gates, experiment plan, failure modes, and criteria for revisiting the approach.
