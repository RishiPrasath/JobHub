# Technical and Responsible AI Research

## Use this instruction for

Evaluating architecture, data, integrations, model boundaries, privacy, security, fairness, reliability, explainability, and user control.

## Assigned team

- **Primary:** Technical Architect for system and operating constraints, or Responsible AI Reviewer when the question is primarily about harm, privacy, fairness, or human control.
- **Support:** The other of those two roles; Quality and Evaluation Engineer for testability; AI Tools Expert for provider and tool trade-offs.
- **Synthesis:** Evidence Synthesizer.
- **Review:** Product Strategist for user impact; add AI SDLC Advisor when the question affects agent workflows.

## Questions to cover

- What data is stored, retained, encrypted, sent to models, or excluded.
- Which operations require live information and which can use preserved evidence.
- How to handle prompt injection, sensitive-information disclosure, improper output handling, excessive agency, and supply-chain risks.
- Where human approval is required for external or consequential actions.
- How factuality, uncertainty, fairness, accessibility, cost, latency, reliability, and quality drift will be evaluated.

## Required output

Produce option and trade-off notes, data-flow or risk findings, controls, evaluation requirements, unresolved concerns, and reversible experiments.
