## Cover prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: editorial cover art for a research presentation about job hunting. Create a sophisticated widescreen 16:9 painterly editorial illustration, quiet realism, visible subtle paper grain, warm off-white and deep midnight navy with restrained burnt orange accents. Scene: a thoughtful adult person with medium brown skin in an ordinary home setting at dusk, looking between a smartphone in hand and an open laptop on a simple table. The person, devices and warm pool of lamp light are confined to the RIGHT HALF. On the LEFT HALF leave broad uncluttered very dark navy negative space for editable presentation typography added later, no text in the image. Show ordinary uncertainty and concentration, not despair or triumphant marketing. Broad soft shapes, strong composition, human and believable, suitable for a high-quality client research briefing. No logos, no readable screen text, no charts, no infographic, no arrows, no interface mockup, no watermarks. This is a fictional illustration, not a documentary photo.

## Preparation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: chapter illustration for an evidence-based job-hunting research briefing. Create a sophisticated widescreen 16:9 painterly editorial illustration with subtle paper grain, warm ivory, deep midnight navy and restrained burnt orange. Top-down close view of a pair of medium-brown hands at a simple desk carefully comparing a printed resume with a job description, handwritten notebook beside it and an open laptop cropped at the top. Printed pages contain only abstract grey text lines, NO readable text. Show deliberate checking and preparation rather than automation magic. All important objects clustered to the RIGHT TWO THIRDS, left third largely plain dark navy, room for slide text added later. Flat but richly textured brushwork, soft dusk light, restrained sophisticated composition, no logos, no charts, no infographic, no arrows, no watermarks, no actual identifiable private information. Fictional explanatory illustration, not documentary evidence.
