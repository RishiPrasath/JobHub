## Queue rules

- Prioritize high-consequence uncertainty that can be reduced cheaply.
- Do not start a broad run without a decision, audience, method, and stopping rule.
- Split a run when it requires materially different agents or evidence types.
- Mark a question blocked only when the missing evidence or authority is explicit.
