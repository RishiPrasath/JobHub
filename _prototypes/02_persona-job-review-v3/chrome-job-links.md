# Chrome job-link inventory

This inventory records links gathered during the V3 preparation pass using the user’s Chrome session. It is test data for the prototype, not a recommendation list.

## Capture evidence

The administrative assistant listing was opened as a public MyCareersFuture detail page and its visible text was captured. The fixture is retained at `data/captures/sg-admin-001.txt`.

The other links were found through public Chrome search results and are retained as link-only fixtures until their full detail text is captured or pasted. The prototype must not treat a search result as a complete job posting.
