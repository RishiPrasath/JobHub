# Scope and Roadmap

## Roadmap principle

The roadmap should follow uncertainty reduction, not a predetermined feature list. Each stage should produce evidence or a usable increment that informs the next stage.

## Proposed stages

### Stage 0 — Strategy and discovery

Validate the problem, user segments, AI-assisted workflows, trust requirements, and product-surface options.

### Stage 1 — Narrow workflow prototype

Prototype one end-to-end job-seeker workflow, such as capturing a posting, preserving its source, and helping the user understand it.

### Stage 2 — Evidence-backed preparation

Test candidate comparison, truthful application preparation, and explainable recommendations using realistic examples.

### Stage 3 — Follow-through workflow

Evaluate saving, cross-device return, application tracking, reminders, and interview preparation.

### Stage 4 — Expansion

Expand industries, markets, integrations, automation, and product surfaces only when research and operating evidence justify the added complexity.

## Scope gates

Before expanding scope, confirm:

- The user problem is real and recurring.
- The workflow is understandable and accessible.
- Evidence and uncertainty are presented responsibly.
- Privacy and security risks are controlled.
- The implementation is testable and maintainable.
- The added capability improves user outcomes rather than only increasing activity volume.
